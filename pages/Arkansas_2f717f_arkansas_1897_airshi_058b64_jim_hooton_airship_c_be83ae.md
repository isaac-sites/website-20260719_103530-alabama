## Introduction

Did Jim Hooton really find a landed airship near Texarkana in April 1897? The short answer is that nobody knows, and the surviving evidence is remarkably thin. Hooton, a railroad conductor working for the Iron Mountain Railroad, told the *[Arkansas]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-arkansas-ufo/' | relative_url }}) Gazette* that he encountered a mysterious airship on the ground while hunting near Texarkana during the nationwide 1896–1897 airship wave. According to his account, he approached the craft, spoke with members of its crew and watched it depart. The story became one of the most famous Arkansas episodes from the airship craze because it involved a named witness, a detailed narrative and even a sketch of the craft. ## What Hooton Said Happened Near Texarkana

According to newspaper accounts published on 20 April 1897, Captain Jim Hooton was in the Texarkana area waiting to collect a locomotive and decided to spend time hunting. While outdoors, he reportedly heard a mechanical noise that reminded him of railway equipment, often described in later summaries as sounding like an air brake or compressed-air apparatus. Curious about the source, he followed the sound and came upon what he believed was a landed airship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.explorepinebluff.com/post/ufo-sightings-in-arkansas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: explorepinebluff.com">[Pine Bluff]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">explorepinebluff.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pine Bluff UFO Sightings in Arkansas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pine BluffUFO Sightings in Arkansas - Explore Pine Bluff28 Sept 2022 — An article in the Arkansas Gazette printed on April 20, 1897, repo...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 20, 1897</span></span></span>

Hooton claimed that several men emerged from the craft. He reportedly asked whether this was the same mysterious airship being discussed across the United States. According to the story, the crew confirmed that it was. The encounter then became even more remarkable: the men allegedly explained aspects of the machine, and after preparations were completed, the craft lifted off and disappeared. Hooton later supplied a sketch of the vehicle for newspaper publication.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com/2015/08/ufos-and-arkansas.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com">[arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arkansas History&#x27;s Mysteries</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Perhaps the most prominent of the sightings in Arkansas...</span></span></span>

What makes the account stand out from many other 1897 reports is that it was not merely a distant light in the sky. Hooton claimed a close-range encounter with both the machine and its operators. In modern UFO terminology, it resembles a “landed craft” report rather than a simple aerial sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com/2015/08/ufos-and-arkansas.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com">[arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arkansas History&#x27;s Mysteries</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Perhaps the most prominent of the sightings in Arkansas...</span></span></span>

## How Railway Details Shaped the Story

One reason the Hooton report attracted attention was the witness's occupation. Railroad workers were widely regarded as practical observers familiar with machinery, schedules and mechanical sounds. Hooton's description reflected that background.

Instead of comparing the sound to something mysterious, he reportedly related it to familiar railway technology. The narrative emphasised mechanical details rather than supernatural ones. The crew were described as ordinary men rather than aliens, and the craft was presented as an advanced but human-built machine. This closely matched the dominant airship narrative circulating in American newspapers during 1897, which often imagined a secret inventor testing a revolutionary flying machine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com/2015/08/ufos-and-arkansas.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com">[arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arkansas History&#x27;s Mysteries</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Perhaps the most prominent of the sightings in Arkansas...</span></span></span>

The railway connection also helped make the story seem plausible to contemporary readers. Late nineteenth-century America was experiencing rapid technological change. Railroads, telegraphs and electrical systems were transforming daily life. Against that backdrop, the idea that an unknown inventor might have achieved powered flight before the rest of the world did not seem entirely impossible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_airship" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mystery airship</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mystery airship</span></span></span>

From a historical perspective, this is one of the most revealing aspects of the case. The account reflects the technological expectations of its era. The mystery machine was not described as extraterrestrial. It was portrayed as the next step in human engineering.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_airship" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mystery airship</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mystery airship</span></span></span>

## Why the Evidence Remains Weak

Despite its vivid details, the Hooton story faces significant evidential problems.

First, virtually everything known about the incident comes from a single newspaper report and later retellings derived from it. Researchers have not uncovered corroborating testimony from other witnesses who saw the landed craft or interacted with its crew.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/ufo-sightings-8576/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: encyclopediaofarkansas.net">[Encyclopedia of Arkansas]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">encyclopediaofarkansas.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo sightings 8576</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Encyclopedia of ArkansasUFO Sightings22 Oct 2025 — By the spring, the phenomenon moved into Arkansas. Second, there was no physical evidence. No photographs, parts, engineering drawings or independently verified records of the alleged airship were produced. Hooton's sketch added colour to the story, but a witness drawing is not proof that the object existed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com/2015/08/ufos-and-arkansas.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com">[arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arkansasstatearchives.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Arkansas History&#x27;s Mysteries</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Perhaps the most prominent of the sightings in Arkansas...</span></span></span>

Third, the reported behaviour of the crew fits a recurring pattern found throughout the 1896–1897 airship wave. Numerous stories from different states featured approachable pilots, conversations with witnesses, mechanical repairs, requests for supplies and explanations that the craft belonged to a secret inventor. Historians of the wave have long noted that many such accounts appear folkloric, humorous or embellished rather than strictly factual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_airship" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mystery airship</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mystery airship</span></span></span>

A further difficulty is that no follow-up investigation ever verified the event.     var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
