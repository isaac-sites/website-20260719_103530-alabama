## Introduction

The exact date of Nick Mariana’s famous [Great Falls]({{ 'great-falls/' | relative_url }}) film may seem like a minor detail, but it has major consequences for how the footage is evaluated. The film itself is real and survives in archival collections. The problem is that investigators have not always agreed on precisely when it was taken. During later reviews of the case, researchers found conflicting references to early August and mid-August 1950, creating uncertainty that affects every attempt to compare the sighting with aircraft movements, weather conditions, military activity and witness records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

## Can the Mariana Film's Date Be Trusted?

Most modern accounts place the filming at around 11:25–11:30 a.m. on 15 August 1950 at Legion Stadium in Great Falls, where Mariana, manager of the Great Falls Selectrics baseball club, and his secretary Virginia Raunig reported seeing two bright objects crossing the sky. Contemporary retellings, local histories and many later investigations repeat that date.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

However, when the University of Colorado UFO study revisited the case in the 1960s, investigators discovered that the timing was not as straightforward as many assumed. Roy Craig and David Saunders found themselves dealing with conflicting information about whether the film had been shot on 5 August or 15 August 1950. The disagreement emerged decades after the event, when memories had faded and records had to be reconstructed from surviving documents.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

That uncertainty became important because the Great Falls film is already difficult to analyse. The objects appear only as bright images without enough detail to reveal their distance, size or shape conclusively. When image evidence is limited, contextual evidence becomes more valuable. A secure date would allow investigators to check other records; an uncertain date weakens those checks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

## How the August 1950 Dates Became Disputed

The date confusion appears to have grown from inconsistencies in later investigations rather than from the original publicity surrounding the sighting. Contemporary accounts generally pointed to mid-August, and newspaper coverage appeared immediately after the reported event. Yet later researchers encountered references that suggested an earlier August date, leading to questions about whether some records had been misremembered or misfiled.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

The dispute illustrates a common problem in historical UFO investigations. Researchers are often forced to compare:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Witness recollections recorded years later.
* Newspaper reports written close to the event.
* Military files compiled during investigations.
* Secondary accounts produced decades afterwards.

</div>

When those sources disagree, even slightly, reconstructing a precise timeline becomes difficult. In the Mariana case, the disagreement was not about whether the filming occurred in August 1950, but about exactly which August day should be used as the foundation for further analysis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

For a case that has been debated for more than seventy years, a ten-day discrepancy is significant. It changes which aircraft were present, which weather reports apply and which local events can be used to verify witness statements.

## What Baseball Schedules and Contemporary Records Reveal

One reason many researchers continue to favour 15 August is that the baseball context aligns well with the traditional account. Mariana was inspecting Legion Stadium before a scheduled game, and numerous later summaries consistently place the event on that date. Local baseball histories and Great Falls sources have preserved the same chronology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.milb.com/news/gcs-350388" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: milb.com">[milb.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">milb.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gcs 350388</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">At 11:25 am on August 15, Nick Mariana, the general manager of the Great Falls &quot;Electrics&quot; minor...Read more...</span></span></span>

Baseball schedules matter because they provide an independent record that is separate from UFO testimony. If a witness claims to have been preparing for a particular game, historians can compare that statement against league schedules, newspaper sports pages and team records. Such documents are often more reliable than decades-old memories because they were created at the time. This is one of the standard ways investigators test historical UFO reports: they look for ordinary records that either support or contradict the claimed timeline.

In the Mariana case, the sporting context offers a valuable anchor. The fact that the witness was performing a routine job connected to a baseball club creates opportunities for cross-checking that do not exist in many UFO cases. Even so, the lingering disagreement over the exact day shows how easily uncertainty can survive when original documentation is incomplete or inconsistently cited.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

## Why Uncertain Timing Limits Aircraft and Weather Checks

The most important consequence of the date dispute is practical. Many explanations proposed for the Mariana film depend on knowing exactly when it was recorded.

The US Air Force initially suggested that the objects were related to F-94 jet aircraft operating in the area. Later investigators questioned that conclusion, and some Air Force analysts eventually admitted that the aircraft explanation was not securely established. Yet testing any aircraft hypothesis requires precise timing. Researchers need to know which flights were airborne, where they were located and whether their position matched the filmed objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

The same problem affects weather analysis. Meteorological records can reveal cloud cover, visibility, wind direction and atmospheric conditions that might help explain unusual observations. But weather data are tied to specific dates and times. If investigators are uncertain whether they should be examining records from 5 August or 15 August, the evidential value of those comparisons decreases.

The issue extends beyond aircraft and weather:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Military flight activity changes from day to day.
* Civil aviation traffic varies by schedule.
* Astronomical conditions change over time.
* Witness movements and routines may differ between dates.

</div>

A mistaken date can therefore make a plausible explanation appear impossible or make an impossible explanation appear plausible.

## Why the Date Question Still Matters Today

The Mariana film remains one of Montana’s most discussed UFO cases because it combines a surviving motion-picture record with named witnesses and official investigations. Yet the date controversy demonstrates an important lesson about how UFO evidence is tested.

The strongest investigations do not focus only on what appears in a photograph or film frame. They also examine the surrounding facts: where the witness stood, what else was in the sky, what official records exist and whether the timeline can be verified independently. In the Great Falls case, uncertainty over the exact date limits the precision of those tests.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_UFO_film" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mariana UFO film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mariana UFO film</span></span></span>

That does not prove the objects were extraordinary, nor does it prove they were ordinary aircraft. Instead, it highlights why historical UFO investigations often remain unresolved.     var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
