## Introduction

The KC-97 tanker theory is one of the most detailed conventional explanations ever proposed for the famous [Exeter]({{ 'exeter/' | relative_url }}) sighting of September 1965. It attempts to explain the witnesses’ unusual description of five bright red lights flashing in sequence by linking them to the lighting system of a Boeing KC-97 Stratofreighter aerial refuelling tanker operating from nearby Pease Air Force Base. The idea is attractive because it addresses a feature that many other explanations struggle with: the distinctive pattern of red lights reported by Norman Muscarello and Exeter police officers Eugene Bertrand and David Hunt. Yet the theory remains controversial because no confirmed flight record has securely placed a KC-97 over the Exeter area at the precise time and location required. As a result, the tanker hypothesis is often viewed as plausible but unproven rather than a definitive solution to the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.centerforinquiry.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2011/11/22164321/p16.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.centerforinquiry.org">[cdn.centerforinquiry.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.centerforinquiry.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter Incident&#x27; Solved!by ACUFO Case · 2011 — A U.S. Air Force KC-97 refueling tanker like this one became an unintentional UFO, sparki...</span></span></span>

## Why the Five-Light Sequence Suggested a KC-97 Tanker

The central strength of the KC-97 explanation is that it focuses on the witnesses’ most unusual observation. Rather than describing a clearly visible craft, the primary witnesses repeatedly emphasised a row of bright red lights that flashed one after another in a sequence.

Retired US Air Force pilot and sceptical investigator James McGaha argued that this pattern resembled the “director” or refuelling guidance lights mounted on KC-97 tankers. During aerial refuelling operations these lights helped guide approaching aircraft. According to McGaha's reconstruction, a witness viewing the underside of a tanker at night could see a line of red lights flashing in sequence. He further suggested that reflections from the tanker’s refuelling boom might have contributed to the impression of a larger object moving or swaying in the darkness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.centerforinquiry.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2011/11/22164321/p16.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.centerforinquiry.org">[cdn.centerforinquiry.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.centerforinquiry.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter Incident&#x27; Solved!by ACUFO Case · 2011 — A U.S. Air Force KC-97 refueling tanker like this one became an unintentional UFO, sparki...</span></span></span>

The theory also draws support from geography. Exeter lay relatively close to Pease Air Force Base, a Strategic Air Command installation that routinely handled tanker and bomber operations during the Cold War. Military aircraft in the area were therefore not unusual. If a KC-97 happened to be operating at low apparent elevation from the witnesses’ perspective, its lighting arrangement could potentially explain why observers saw a dramatic display without clearly seeing a solid aircraft structure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.centerforinquiry.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2011/11/22164321/p16.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.centerforinquiry.org">[cdn.centerforinquiry.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.centerforinquiry.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter Incident&#x27; Solved!by ACUFO Case · 2011 — A U.S. Air Force KC-97 refueling tanker like this one became an unintentional UFO, sparki...</span></span></span>

For supporters of the tanker explanation, this is an important point. Many UFO reports involve lights whose apparent size, speed and distance become difficult to judge in darkness. A brightly illuminated aircraft viewed without obvious reference points can appear far closer, larger or stranger than it really is.

## Problems of Timing, Sound, Distance and Flight-Path Reconstruction

Although the KC-97 theory offers a specific mechanism, critics argue that it encounters significant practical difficulties when compared with the witness testimony.

One issue concerns apparent altitude and distance. Muscarello and the police officers believed the lights approached quite closely. Officer Bertrand later described reacting as though an object was passing overhead at relatively low altitude. The tanker theory requires the witnesses to have substantially misjudged distance, interpreting lights thousands of feet away as something much nearer. Such errors are certainly possible at night, but the explanation depends heavily upon them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nhuforesearch.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nhuforesearch.wordpress.com">[New Hampshire UFO Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nhuforesearch.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wordpress.com.</span></span></span>

Another difficulty involves sound. A KC-97 was a large four-engine propeller aircraft. Witnesses described an experience dominated by brilliant lights and, in many retellings, little or no obvious engine noise. Advocates of the tanker theory note that sound can be deceptive at night and may not travel predictably depending on atmospheric conditions. Critics respond that a large aircraft operating close enough to create the dramatic impressions reported should normally have produced a more recognisable acoustic signature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nhuforesearch.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nhuforesearch.wordpress.com">[New Hampshire UFO Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nhuforesearch.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wordpress.com.</span></span></span>

Flight-path reconstruction creates further uncertainty. To work, the theory requires a tanker to be positioned at the correct bearing, altitude and time while displaying the relevant lighting configuration. The witnesses reported repeated observations over a period of time rather than a brief glimpse. Reconstructing exactly what an aircraft would have looked like from their changing positions has therefore proven difficult decades after the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.martinshough.com/aerialphenomena/EXETER%20N.H.%20Sep%202-3%201965.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: martinshough.com">[martinshough.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">martinshough.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Exeunt Exeter?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhysicsUFO as a description of five red flashing &quot;receiver lights&quot; (director lights) on the belly of a. The theory's critics also note that the police officers were not completely unfamiliar with aircraft. Bertrand had military experience, and sceptics and believers alike have debated whether such experience makes a major misidentification less likely. That point alone does not rule out error, but it complicates claims that the witnesses simply mistook ordinary aircraft lights for something extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nhuforesearch.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nhuforesearch.wordpress.com">[New Hampshire UFO Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nhuforesearch.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wordpress.com.</span></span></span>

## What the Missing Aircraft Record Means

The most persistent weakness of the KC-97 explanation is not the lighting mechanism itself but the lack of definitive documentation.

The theory emerged decades after the original incident and relies partly on the fact that KC-97 tankers were known to operate from Pease. However, researchers have not produced a flight record that conclusively places a specific tanker over the Exeter area at the precise time needed to match the witnesses' observations. Even some sceptical discussions of the case acknowledge that the reconstruction depends on inference rather than a documented aircraft track.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.centerforinquiry.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2011/11/22164321/p16.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.centerforinquiry.org">[cdn.centerforinquiry.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.centerforinquiry.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter Incident&#x27; Solved!by ACUFO Case · 2011 — A U.S. Air Force KC-97 refueling tanker like this one became an unintentional UFO, sparki...</span></span></span>

This absence matters because the Exeter case was investigated extensively by Project Blue Book and military authorities. Records show that investigators examined military activity in the region, including bomber and training operations. Yet no surviving document has emerged that definitively identifies a KC-97 as the aircraft seen by the witnesses. Critics of the tanker theory argue that if such evidence existed, it would likely have been highlighted long ago as a straightforward resolution to one of the most famous cases in [New Hampshire]({{ 'why-new-hampshire-became-a-ufo-landmark/' | relative_url }}) UFO history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nhuforesearch.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nhuforesearch.wordpress.com">[New Hampshire UFO Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nhuforesearch.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wordpress.com.</span></span></span>

At the same time, missing records are not proof that no tanker was present. Military documentation from the Cold War era is incomplete, and not every flight record survived. The absence of confirmation therefore weakens the theory without automatically disproving it.

        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
