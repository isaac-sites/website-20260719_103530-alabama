## Introduction

The illuminated-kite explanation became the leading conventional interpretation of the blue object filmed over Oahu on 29 December 2020 because it addresses several features visible in the footage at the same time: the object's changing shape, its apparent lack of engine noise, its steady descent and the way it appeared as a bright blue form without any clearly visible structure attached to it. Rather than requiring a large powered craft, the theory proposes a lightweight kite carrying blue LED lights or an illuminated tail. At night, the fabric, frame and flying line can disappear against a dark sky, leaving only the lights visible to observers and cameras. Later video analysis strengthened this possibility by showing that the object's apparent shape was less rigid than many viewers initially assumed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

## Why the blue shape resembles flexible LED kite lighting

A central question raised by the Oahu videos is why the object appears elongated, curved and variable in shape rather than consistently solid. Supporters of the kite explanation argue that this behaviour is exactly what would be expected from LED lighting attached to a flexible airborne object. Modern display kites often use strips of LEDs running along a tail, spar or suspension line. When viewed at night, the illuminated portions remain visible while the darker supporting material effectively disappears.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

Several characteristics of the footage fit this interpretation:

* The blue form appears stretched rather than compact.
* Its outline changes gradually instead of maintaining a fixed geometry.
* Different parts of the illuminated shape seem to brighten or fade as viewing angles change.
* The object does not display obvious navigation lights or other features normally associated with aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

Reviewers comparing the Oahu footage with recordings of illuminated kites noted that LED-equipped kites can create surprisingly unusual visual effects. A flexible light strip moving in wind can appear to bend, twist or alter length even when the underlying object remains relatively stable. Under low-light conditions, smartphone cameras often exaggerate these effects through motion blur and overexposure around bright light sources.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

This does not prove the object was a kite, but it explains why witnesses might perceive a single glowing craft when the camera is actually recording only the illuminated portions of a lightweight flying object.

## How stabilised footage weakened the rigid-craft claim

The strongest challenge to the original “large craft” interpretation came from later examination of the videos themselves. Early viewers often focused on the apparent size of the blue object and the impression that it was travelling through the sky as a coherent vehicle. However, detailed frame-by-frame reviews suggested that much of this impression depended on camera movement rather than object movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

Sceptical investigator Mick West stabilised portions of the footage and argued that the object looked less like a rigid machine once camera shake was removed. In the stabilised view, the illuminated shape appeared more flexible and irregular, resembling a line or cluster of lights whose form changed over time. Rather than revealing a structured craft, the processing reduced some of the dramatic motion that viewers initially associated with a manoeuvring vehicle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

## Why the descent and silence fit a kite better than a powered aircraft

Witnesses reported that the blue object appeared to descend toward the ocean, a detail that initially encouraged speculation about a crash or controlled landing. The footage itself, however, does not provide reliable distance measurements, making it difficult to determine how far away the object was or how quickly it was descending.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Not because I thought it was an (alien) UFO...but because I like to use clips like this for demo...</span></span></span>

A kite interpretation offers a straightforward mechanism. If an illuminated kite lost lift, encountered changing wind conditions or broke free from its operator, it could gradually drift downward while remaining visible as a bright blue light.
