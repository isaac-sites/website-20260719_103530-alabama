## Introduction

The quickest way to understand why the [Twin Falls saucer]({{ 'twin-falls-hoax/' | relative_url }}) is regarded as a solved case is to look at the object itself. Found in a Twin Falls garden on 11 July 1947, the disc briefly attracted police, FBI and Army attention during the height of America's first flying-saucer craze. Yet the physical evidence pointed not to advanced technology but to a homemade construction assembled from discarded household and radio components. ## The Disc's Materials and Homemade Construction

Descriptions published in contemporary newspapers and later summaries show that the Twin Falls object was designed to look technological rather than functional. It was roughly 30 inches (about 76–77 centimetres) across and resembled two shallow metal discs joined together. One side was painted silver, the other gold, and a transparent dome sat on top, held in place with visible bolts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Falls_saucer_hoax" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span></span></span>

The design exploited popular expectations in July 1947. Newspapers were full of reports about mysterious flying discs, and many members of the public associated advanced aircraft with visible electrical equipment. A casual observer seeing wires, tubes and a metallic disc could easily interpret the object as a piece of unusual technology. Investigators, however, saw something different: ordinary components assembled for appearance rather than engineering purpose. The exposed bolts, damaged wiring and readily identifiable radio parts suggested fabrication from available scrap rather than manufacture by any unknown aerospace source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Falls_saucer_hoax" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span></span></span>

An important point is that the object did not merely look crude in hindsight. Contemporary investigators recognised familiar household and radio materials almost immediately. The uncertainty came not from the object's sophistication but from the fact that authorities wanted to verify what it was before making a public statement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_reports_and_disinformation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO reports and disinformation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO reports and disinformation</span></span></span>

## How the Garden Scene Imitated a Crash

The success of the prank depended as much on the setting as on the object itself. The disc was found in a residential garden, where disturbed ground made it appear that something had struck the lawn and slid across the surface. Residents also reported hearing a noise during the night, later remembered as a thud. These details helped create the impression of a recent impact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Falls_saucer_hoax" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span></span></span>

Several features of the scene encouraged people to think in terms of a crash:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The object appeared suddenly overnight.
* The lawn showed marks consistent with movement across the ground.
* Witnesses recalled hearing a noise hours before the discovery.
* The national press was already saturated with flying-saucer stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Falls_saucer_hoax" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span></span></span>

</div>

Yet the crash narrative had weaknesses. Reports noted that telephone and power lines crossed the area. If a large object had genuinely fallen from the sky into the garden, it would have been difficult to explain how it avoided striking those obstacles. That inconsistency became one of several clues that the scene had been staged rather than created by an uncontrolled descent.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.project1947.com/roswell/tfalls.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[project1947.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Twin Falls, Idaho</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 12, 1947Shortly after their departure, McCracken announced the whole thing was a hoax. The boys told officers they used parts of an...</span></span></span>

The prank's effectiveness therefore came from combining a visually striking object with environmental cues that encouraged observers to imagine a dramatic arrival from the sky. Neither element alone would have been as persuasive.

## The Confession and Evidence That Settled the Case

The decisive evidence came when investigators traced the object's origin to local youths. Contemporary accounts reported that four teenage boys confessed to constructing and planting the disc. According to those reports, they told officers that they had assembled the object from discarded parts and placed it in the garden the evening before it was discovered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Falls_saucer_hoax" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Twin Falls saucer hoax</span></span></span>

      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
