## Introduction

[South Carolina]({{ 'south-carolina/' | relative_url }})'s UFO report totals are useful, but only if they are interpreted carefully. Civilian reporting databases such as the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC) and the Mutual UFO Network (MUFON) show where people have reported unusual objects or lights, not where unexplained aerial phenomena have been proven to occur. The numbers therefore reflect both what witnesses believed they saw and how, when and where they chose to submit a report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/databank/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[National UFO Reporting Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National UFO Reporting Center Data Bank &#124; NUFORC</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National UFO Reporting CenterData Bank &#124; NUFORC - Latest UFO SightingsSeptember 6, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 6, 2021</span></span></span>

## How civilian sighting databases collect and publish reports

The two civilian databases most frequently used in discussions of South Carolina sightings are NUFORC and MUFON. Both rely primarily on voluntary witness submissions rather than systematic observation programmes.

NUFORC has accepted reports since the 1970s and maintains one of the largest publicly searchable archives of UFO reports. Witnesses submit descriptions of what they observed, including the date, approximate location, duration and appearance of the object. The organisation states openly that reports are generally published in the witness's own words, that obvious hoaxes are filtered where possible, and that the database should not be taken as proof that every event occurred as described. Recent reports receive an internal assessment ranging from likely explainable to highly anomalous, while older reports remain largely ungraded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/databank/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[National UFO Reporting Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National UFO Reporting Center Data Bank &#124; NUFORC</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National UFO Reporting CenterData Bank &#124; NUFORC - Latest UFO SightingsSeptember 6, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 6, 2021</span></span></span>

MUFON also accepts public reports, but follows a different model by assigning many cases to volunteer field investigators. Even so, not every report receives a full investigation, and access to the complete database is more limited than NUFORC's public archive. Researchers comparing reporting systems note that both organisations contain a mixture of unexplained cases, ordinary misidentifications and reports with too little information for firm conclusions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.narcap.org/faa-reporting-recommendation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: narcap.org">[narcap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">narcap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Platte, John-Michael Guttierez, Ted Roe, Ryan Graves, 2023 — National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena...</span></span></span>

For South Carolina, this means that published totals combine reports of widely differing quality. A brief account of distant lights over [Myrtle Beach]({{ 'myrtle-beach/' | relative_url }}) and a detailed multi-witness observation near Charleston each count as one report despite offering very different evidential value.

## Why place names, duplicate accounts and delayed submissions matter

Raw totals can easily be distorted by the way reports are entered and organised.

One of the biggest issues is geographical consistency. Witnesses may identify a nearby city rather than the exact observation point, especially along the Grand Strand where municipal boundaries between Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach and surrounding communities are not always obvious to visitors. Reports from offshore, beaches or highways may therefore be assigned to different places even when describing the same event.

Duplicate reporting is another challenge. Several observers watching the same object may each submit separate reports, while the same witness may report an event to both NUFORC and MUFON. Unless researchers compare times, locations and descriptions carefully, a single incident can appear multiple times in broader datasets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.narcap.org/faa-reporting-recommendation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: narcap.org">[narcap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">narcap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Platte, John-Michael Guttierez, Ted Roe, Ryan Graves, 2023 — National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena...</span></span></span>

Reporting delays also affect interpretation. Some witnesses submit accounts within hours, while others wait months or even years. NUFORC allows searches by both event date and posting date because those timelines often differ substantially. A sudden increase in reports posted during one month may therefore reflect people uploading older experiences rather than a genuine surge in sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/ndx/?id=event" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[National UFO Reporting Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National UFO Reporting Center NUFORC Reports by Month</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National UFO Reporting CenterNUFORC Reports by MonthAugust 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 20, 2023</span></span></span>

These limitations do not make the databases unreliable. Instead, they remind researchers that the totals represent accumulated witness reports rather than a precise real-time record of unusual aerial activity.

## How to separate reporting clusters from confirmed anomalies

Large numbers of reports deserve attention, but they are only the starting point for analysis.

A stronger assessment asks additional questions:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Were several independent witnesses observing the same event?**
* **Do descriptions broadly agree on time, direction and appearance?**
* **Are there supporting photographs, radar information or aviation records?**
* **Could ordinary aircraft, satellites, astronomical objects or launches explain the observations?**
* **Was the report investigated beyond the initial witness statement?**

</div>

This approach often changes the significance of apparent clusters.

For example, South Carolina's busiest reporting areas are also among its busiest aviation and tourism centres. Myrtle Beach combines heavy tourist numbers with open coastal views, while Charleston sits beside a major commercial airport and military aviation facilities. Those environments increase opportunities to observe unfamiliar lights without necessarily increasing the number of genuinely unexplained phenomena.

Modern reporting organisations themselves encourage witnesses to eliminate common causes before filing a report. NUFORC specifically advises checking for Starlink satellite trains, bright planets, camera artefacts and other well-known sources of misidentification before submitting an observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/report-a-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[National UFO Reporting Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">report a ufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National UFO Reporting CenterFile a UFO Report &#124; NUFORCSeptember 13, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 13, 2021</span></span></span>

Consequently, a concentration of reports should be treated as a signal that a location deserves closer examination, not as evidence that extraordinary events are occurring there.

## What South Carolina's totals really tell us

Viewed carefully, South Carolina's civilian reporting data reveal several useful patterns.

First, the databases confirm that people consistently report unusual aerial observations across the state rather than in only one isolated location. Coastal communities, larger cities and areas with heavy aviation activity appear repeatedly because they provide many opportunities for people to notice unfamiliar objects.

Second, the data show how strongly reporting behaviour influences apparent hotspots. Population density, tourism, public awareness of reporting systems and media attention all affect whether an observation becomes part of the historical record.

Finally, the databases demonstrate why individual case quality matters more than raw totals. Hundreds of brief reports do not necessarily outweigh a smaller number of well-documented incidents supported by multiple witnesses or independent evidence.

For anyone studying South Carolina's UFO history, civilian reporting databases are therefore best understood as valuable archives of public testimony.   function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
