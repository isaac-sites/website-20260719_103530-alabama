## Introduction

The Asheville School sighting of May 1964 is often remembered for the unusually detailed sketches produced by two pupils and preserved in the Project Blue Book files. Those drawings are valuable evidence because they show what the students believed they saw and how they attempted to record it. However, they also highlight a central problem in many UFO reports: a witness can draw an object's apparent appearance and movement without being able to measure its true distance, size, altitude or speed. The Asheville sketches therefore strengthen the historical record of the sighting while simultaneously revealing the limits of eyewitness estimation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theprojectbluebookarchive.org/archive/Sanitized%20Version%20of%20Project%20Blue%20Book%20Case%20Files-PDFs-Part5/302532744.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theprojectbluebookarchive.org">[theprojectbluebookarchive.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theprojectbluebookarchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DATE - The Project Blue Book ArchiveUFO Sighting, Asheville, North Carolina, 9 May 64. ## What the students drew and described

The surviving Blue Book file contains a sequence of sketches showing an object moving across the sky near the Asheville School campus before dawn on 9 May 1964. The drawings place the object in relation to a prominent landmark, the school's [water tower]({{ 'water-tower/' | relative_url }}), and depict changes in orientation as it appeared to slow, manoeuvre and then depart. The witnesses also supplied written descriptions, including claims about apparent rotation, coloured sections and material that seemed to fall from the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theprojectbluebookarchive.org/archive/Sanitized%20Version%20of%20Project%20Blue%20Book%20Case%20Files-PDFs-Part5/302532744.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theprojectbluebookarchive.org">[theprojectbluebookarchive.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theprojectbluebookarchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DATE - The Project Blue Book ArchiveUFO Sighting, Asheville, North Carolina, 9 May 64. What makes the sketches notable is their level of detail. Rather than simply reporting a light in the sky, the students attempted to reconstruct a sequence of events. For historians of [North Carolina]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-north-carolinas/' | relative_url }}) UFO reports, that is useful because it provides a contemporaneous record rather than a recollection given years later. The drawings help establish that the witnesses were trying to document what they observed rather than merely repeating a rumour.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theprojectbluebookarchive.org/archive/Sanitized%20Version%20of%20Project%20Blue%20Book%20Case%20Files-PDFs-Part5/302532744.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theprojectbluebookarchive.org">[theprojectbluebookarchive.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theprojectbluebookarchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DATE - The Project Blue Book ArchiveUFO Sighting, Asheville, North Carolina, 9 May 64. Yet the sketches record appearance, not measurement. The object is shown from the witnesses' viewpoint, but the drawings do not establish how far away it was. Without a known distance, even a careful sketch cannot reveal the object's actual dimensions or flight characteristics.

## Why distance changes estimates of speed and altitude

The most important limitation in the Asheville account is the absence of a reliable range estimate. Human observers can judge whether something appears above a landmark such as a water tower, but they are much less reliable at determining how far beyond that landmark the object actually is.

This creates several problems:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Altitude estimates become uncertain.** A witness may report an object at 12,000 feet, but unless the distance is known independently, that figure is largely an impression rather than a measured value.
* **Speed estimates become uncertain.** The same apparent motion could represent a nearby object moving slowly or a distant object moving very quickly.
* **Size estimates become uncertain.** A small object close to the observer and a large object far away can produce a similar visual impression.
* **Manoeuvres can be exaggerated by perspective.** Changes in viewing angle may create the appearance of sudden turns or accelerations that are difficult to quantify from the ground.

</div>

Investigators have long recognised this problem in aerial observations. An object's angular movement across the sky can be observed directly, but converting that apparent movement into true speed requires knowledge of distance. Without that information, calculations quickly become speculative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://kirkmcd.princeton.edu/JEMcDonald/bloecher_67.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kirkmcd.princeton.edu">[kirkmcd.princeton.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kirkmcd.princeton.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">bloecher 67</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Report on the UFO Wave of 1947by T Bloecher · 1967 · Cited by 47 — UFO witnesses in the course of my recent study that I have evidently o...</span></span></span>

In the Asheville case, the students' estimates of extraordinary speed depended on assumptions about how far away the object was. If those assumptions were wrong, the inferred performance of the object would change dramatically.

### The water tower as a reference point

The sketches frequently use the school water tower as a visual marker. This gives readers a useful sense of where the witnesses were looking, but it does not solve the distance problem.

An object appearing above or beside a tower might actually be:

* relatively close to the tower;
* many miles beyond it;
* or even aligned with it only from the witnesses' particular viewpoint.

The drawings therefore establish a line of sight rather than a precise three-dimensional position. This distinction is crucial when evaluating claims about altitude, hovering or sudden acceleration.

## What the sketches prove and what they cannot

The Asheville drawings remain important evidence because they preserve details that might otherwise have been lost. They demonstrate that the witnesses reported a structured observation, attempted to document motion over time and submitted their material to official investigators. The existence of the sketches also confirms that Project Blue Book received and archived the report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theprojectbluebookarchive.org/archive/Sanitized%20Version%20of%20Project%20Blue%20Book%20Case%20Files-PDFs-Part5/302532744.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theprojectbluebookarchive.org">[theprojectbluebookarchive.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theprojectbluebookarchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DATE - The Project Blue Book ArchiveUFO Sighting, Asheville, North Carolina, 9 May 64. However, the sketches do not prove several claims often associated with the case:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* They do not establish the object's true size.
* They do not establish its actual altitude.
* They do not establish its real speed.
* They do not prove that the object performed physically impossible manoeuvres.
* They do not identify the object as an aircraft, planet, unknown craft or anything else with certainty.

</div>

This distinction helps explain why official investigators could accept that the students sincerely reported an unusual sight while remaining unconvinced that the evidence demonstrated an extraordinary vehicle. Blue Book's review considered conventional explanations, including astronomical objects and aircraft, and did not treat the drawings themselves as measurements capable of resolving the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theprojectbluebookarchive.org/archive/Sanitized%20Version%20of%20Project%20Blue%20Book%20Case%20Files-PDFs-Part5/302532744.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theprojectbluebookarchive.org">[theprojectbluebookarchive.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theprojectbluebookarchive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DATE - The Project Blue Book ArchiveUFO Sighting, Asheville, North Carolina, 9 May 64. ## Why the drawings still matter in North Carolina UFO history

The Asheville School sketches occupy an unusual place in North Carolina UFO history. Many sighting reports survive only as brief newspaper summaries or second-hand retellings.     var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
