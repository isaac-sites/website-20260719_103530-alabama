## Introduction

The short answer is that nobody knows for certain whether the blue object seen over Oahu on 29 December 2020 actually entered the Pacific Ocean. Witnesses consistently reported that it appeared to descend toward the water, and several people told police and reporters that they saw it drop into the sea. However, the available videos do not show a clear impact, splash, debris field, or any other evidence that would confirm an ocean entry. The case is therefore best described as a reported ocean descent rather than a documented crash into the water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

## What Witnesses Believed They Saw Near the Shoreline

Accounts collected by local news outlets described a bright blue object moving across the sky over leeward Oahu before descending toward the coast. Witnesses followed it by car and reported that it appeared to come down near the water. Some 911 callers specifically stated that the object seemed to fall into the ocean, prompting police notifications and inquiries to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

From the perspective of the observers, the conclusion was understandable. The object appeared low in the sky, moved toward the shoreline, and eventually disappeared from view. In darkness, people naturally interpret a descending light that vanishes near the horizon as something that has gone into the sea. That interpretation became part of the story almost immediately and was repeated in many later reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

What is important, however, is the difference between appearance and measurement. Witnesses described what the event looked like from their position on the ground. They were not in a position to determine the object's exact distance, altitude, or final location. Those quantities would require additional data that were never publicly established.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

## Why the Footage Cannot Establish Distance or Impact

The strongest limitation of the available videos is that they record only a bright light against a dark background. At night, a camera captures the direction of a light source but provides very little information about how far away it is.

Several key pieces of information are missing:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* No verified distance to the object.
* No synchronised multi-camera triangulation.
* No visible splash or impact plume.
* No recovered debris.
* No radar track showing a descent into the ocean.
* No confirmed location where the object entered the water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

Without those reference points, an apparent descent can be misleading. A relatively small object moving downward nearby can look similar to a much larger object farther away. Likewise, an object disappearing behind terrain, clouds, or the horizon can appear to enter the sea even if it never reaches the water. These are well-known problems in interpreting night-time aerial footage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Actually, I would stop and take video of it. ## The Problem of the Missing Splash

If the object truly entered the ocean, many readers expect that a splash should have been visible. The footage does not provide such confirmation.

This does not prove that the object stayed out of the water. A distant splash at night might be difficult or impossible to record on a mobile phone. However, the absence of any visible impact means that the videos cannot be used as proof of ocean entry. They only show the light descending and then disappearing from view.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

The distinction may seem minor, but it is crucial. A video that clearly shows a splash, impact flash, debris field or recovery effort would support a much stronger claim. The Oahu recordings do not contain those features. As a result, investigators are left with testimony that the object seemed to enter the sea rather than direct visual confirmation that it did.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

## What Later Analysis Suggested

As discussion of the case spread, some investigators proposed that the blue object might have been a lighted kite or another lightweight object carrying blue LEDs. That interpretation gained attention because it could explain the elongated shape, changing outline and apparently gentle descent visible in parts of the footage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/blue-ufo-over-hawaii.11526/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[metabunk.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">blue ufo over hawaii.11526</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue UFO Over Hawaii3 Jan 2021 — Actually, I would stop and take video of it. A kite losing lift, drifting offshore, or descending behind the horizon could create the impression that it entered the water. ## What Missing Evidence Would Confirm an Ocean Entry

For the claim that the blue object entered the ocean to move from witness belief to documented fact, investigators would need evidence beyond the existing videos.

The most useful forms of confirmation would include:

* Radar data showing a descending target reaching sea level.
* Coast Guard, police or marine observations of an impact location.
* A clearly visible splash or impact plume in the footage.
* Recovery of debris from a known crash site.
* Photographs or video taken from a second angle that independently confirm water entry.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2020/12/31/faa-notified-after-mysterious-ufo-seen-above-oahu-appeared-drop-into-ocean/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>
