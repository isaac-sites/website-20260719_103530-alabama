## Introduction

In late 2019 and early 2020, eastern [Colorado]({{ 'what-colorados-ufo-history-really/' | relative_url }}) became the centre of one of the most unusual aviation mysteries in recent American history. Residents across the state's north-eastern plains reported seeing groups of bright, aircraft-sized drones flying at night in apparent patterns. Local sheriffs, the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), the FBI, state agencies and military representatives all became involved. Yet after weeks of investigation, no operator was identified, no command centre was found, and no conclusive explanation emerged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://abcnews.com/US/drone-task-force-investigating-mysterious-sightings-colorado-nebraska/story?id=68102066" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abcnews.com">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abcnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsNew drone task force investigating mysterious sightings...A multi-agency task force has been set up to investigate mysterious re...</span></span></span>

## How the sightings spread across eastern Colorado

The first widely publicised reports emerged in December 2019 from Phillips and Yuma counties in north-eastern Colorado. Witnesses described lights moving slowly across the night sky, often between roughly 6 p.m. and 10 p.m. Some observers claimed to see formations of multiple drones flying in grid-like patterns over rural farmland. Estimates varied, but reports sometimes mentioned groups of six to ten craft and, in a few cases, considerably more.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Colorado_drone_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span></span></span>

What made the reports unusual was not a single dramatic encounter but their persistence. Similar sightings were reported across several counties and into neighbouring Nebraska. Local law-enforcement agencies received enough calls that the matter quickly moved beyond isolated witness accounts. Sheriffs publicly acknowledged that something was being seen, even though they could not determine exactly what it was.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2020/jan/02/colorado-drones-nebraska-faa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[theguardian.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">colorado drones nebraska faa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianRural Colorado residents &#x27;nervous&#x27; as mysterious drones...Jan 3, 2020 — Residents of rural Colorado have been left “very ner...</span></span></span>

Descriptions often suggested relatively large drones, sometimes estimated at around six feet (1.8 metres) across. Witnesses reported blinking navigation lights and flight patterns that appeared organised rather than random. The impression of coordination fuelled speculation that a commercial, government or military operator might be involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Colorado_drone_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span></span></span>

The story gained national attention as media coverage expanded. Public concern grew because the flights appeared to occur repeatedly over sparsely populated agricultural areas, yet no organisation stepped forward to claim responsibility.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2020/jan/02/colorado-drones-nebraska-faa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">colorado drones nebraska faa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianRural Colorado residents &#x27;nervous&#x27; as mysterious drones...Jan 3, 2020 — Residents of rural Colorado have been left “very ner...</span></span></span>

## What police, aviation and military agencies checked

By early January 2020, the reports had escalated into a multi-agency investigation. More than seventy representatives from local, state and federal organisations attended a coordination meeting in Brush, Colorado. Participants included law-enforcement agencies, the FAA, the FBI and military representatives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://abcnews.com/US/drone-task-force-investigating-mysterious-sightings-colorado-nebraska/story?id=68102066" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abcnews.com">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abcnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsNew drone task force investigating mysterious sightings...A multi-agency task force has been set up to investigate mysterious re...</span></span></span>

Investigators explored several possibilities:

The FAA contacted known drone companies and authorised testing sites in the region but reported that none appeared responsible. Military officials also stated that they were not conducting the reported flights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Colorado_drone_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span></span></span>

One of the most publicised developments was a search for a suspected "command vehicle" that might be controlling multiple drones from the ground. Law-enforcement officers circulated alerts and looked for such a vehicle across the eastern plains. The search produced no confirmed target and was eventually called off.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Colorado_drone_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span></span></span>

Colorado authorities also deployed aerial resources. The Colorado Division of Fire Prevention and Control used a Pilatus PC-12 surveillance aircraft equipped for observation missions in an attempt to locate or track the reported drones. Even with additional airborne monitoring, investigators failed to identify a controlling operator.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Colorado_drone_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span></span></span>

## Why radar and tracking data never solved the mystery

For readers interested in military radar and aviation reports, one of the most striking aspects of the case is what investigators did not find.

Despite weeks of attention, no publicly released radar record demonstrated a coordinated fleet moving across eastern Colorado. No definitive tracking data emerged showing dozens of drones operating simultaneously. Likewise, investigators never released clear imagery proving that all reported lights represented a single organised drone operation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.kunc.org/news/2021-07-25/documents-indicate-how-little-officials-knew-about-mysterious-drones-last-year" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kunc.org">[KUNC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kunc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">documents indicate how little officials knew about mysterious drones last year</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Documents Indicate How Little Officials Knew About...25 Jul 2021 — 13, 2020), the Colorado Department of Homeland Security issued a...</span></span></span>

This absence of evidence does not necessarily mean witnesses saw nothing. Small drones can be difficult to track, especially at low altitude and in rural terrain. Radar systems designed for conventional aircraft are not always optimised for detecting numerous small unmanned vehicles. Aviation specialists have long noted that drone detection can be technically challenging, particularly when objects are small, slow and close to the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10038" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

However, the lack of supporting sensor data became increasingly important as the investigation continued. A genuine fleet operating nightly over multiple counties might be expected to leave a stronger trail of photographs, radar returns, telemetry, recovered equipment or identified operators. The result was a gap between witness testimony and measurable evidence. Residents continued reporting unusual lights, but investigators struggled to transform those reports into a documented, trackable operation.

## The growing debate over what people were actually seeing

As the investigation progressed, sceptical explanations gained attention.

Several aviation observers argued that at least some sightings were likely conventional aircraft. At night, distant aeroplanes approaching an observer can appear stationary before seeming to drift sideways or change direction. Bright landing lights can create the impression of hovering objects, particularly over the flat landscapes of eastern Colorado.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Colorado_drone_sightings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2019–20 Colorado drone sightings</span></span></span>

      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
