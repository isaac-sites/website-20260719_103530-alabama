## Introduction

One of the most important but often overlooked features of the April 1975 [Lumberton UFO wave]({{ 'lumberton-wave/' | relative_url }}) is the role played by police radio communications. Reports were not isolated observations made by people unaware of one another. Instead, officers across parts of south-eastern [North Carolina]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-north-carolinas/' | relative_url }}) relayed sightings in real time through law-enforcement radio networks, allowing patrol units in different towns to watch for the same object and compare what they were seeing. Contemporary investigations treated this radio traffic as evidence that the events unfolded in a traceable sequence across multiple locations. At the same time, the same communications created a potential source of witness influence: once officers heard descriptions from colleagues, later observers may have interpreted ambiguous lights through that shared framework. The radio system therefore strengthens the chronology of the wave while also introducing an important caution when assessing witness independence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://search.worldcat.org/title/The-Lumberton-report-%3A-UFO-activity-in-southern-North-Carolina-April-3-9-1975/oclc/4881106" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: search.worldcat.org">[WorldCat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">search.worldcat.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO activity in southern North Carolina, April 3-9, 1975The Lumberton report: UFO activity in southern North Carolina, April 3-9...</span></span></span>

## How Reports Moved Between Lumberton and Nearby Towns

Accounts collected in *The Lumberton Report* describe sightings spreading beyond Lumberton itself during the first week of April 1975. Officers who believed they were tracking an unusual aerial object reportedly used police radio channels to alert colleagues in neighbouring jurisdictions. This communication pattern matters because it created a rough observational chain. Rather than discovering an object independently and only comparing notes later, officers were often responding to information already circulating through dispatch networks. In practical terms, a patrol officer hearing a call about an unusual light would naturally leave the vehicle, scan the indicated direction and attempt to confirm or reject the report.

For investigators sympathetic to the case, this sequence suggested that the same object was being followed over a broad area. For sceptical analysts, however, it raised a different possibility: radio alerts could focus attention on ordinary lights that might otherwise have gone unnoticed. Both interpretations depend on the same underlying fact—the sightings were linked through communication rather than occurring in complete isolation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://s3images.coroflot.com/user_files/individual_files/original_pdf_221275_cenkof8pltjfkxtu3zp3xxmx0.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: s3images.coroflot.com">[Coroflot]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">s3images.coroflot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">original pdf 221275 cenkof8pltjfkxtu3zp3xxmx0</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Triangular UFOs: An Estimate of the SituationOctober 11, 2021 — Reports of UFOs range from little balls of light, often high in t...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2021</span></span></span>

## What Radio Traffic Adds to the Timeline

The strongest value of the police radio reports is chronological rather than dramatic. Real-time communications can help establish when observations occurred, who was aware of them, and how reports spread geographically.

In many UFO cases, witness accounts are reconstructed days, months or even years after the event. The Lumberton wave differs because officers were exchanging information as events unfolded. Even where complete recordings are unavailable, the surviving investigative summaries indicate that patrol units were responding to ongoing reports rather than recounting a distant memory. This gives researchers a clearer sequence of events than is available in many civilian-only UFO cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://search.worldcat.org/title/The-Lumberton-report-%3A-UFO-activity-in-southern-North-Carolina-April-3-9-1975/oclc/4881106" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: search.worldcat.org">[WorldCat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">search.worldcat.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO activity in southern North Carolina, April 3-9, 1975The Lumberton report: UFO activity in southern North Carolina, April 3-9...</span></span></span>

The radio network also helps explain why law-enforcement witnesses appeared in clusters. The concentration of police reports was not necessarily evidence that officers were uniquely likely to encounter unusual aerial phenomena. Patrol officers were mobile, worked overnight shifts and had immediate access to communications linking multiple jurisdictions. Once one officer reported an unusual light, many others were in a position to look for it within minutes.

This distinction is important. The radio traffic strengthens claims that something was being reported across a region at roughly the same time, but it does not automatically prove that each observation was fully independent.

## How Shared Descriptions May Have Influenced Later Accounts

The central criticism of the radio-linked sightings concerns witness expectation.

Psychologists and investigators have long recognised that people observing an ambiguous stimulus can be influenced by prior information. If an officer hears a colleague describe a large silent craft, a triangular shape or bright lights near the horizon, that description may affect what features are noticed later. The observer is not necessarily inventing details; rather, expectations can shape perception and memory.

The Lumberton wave presents exactly this challenge. Officers were often hearing reports before making their own observations. As a result, some consistency between accounts may reflect genuine similarities in what was seen, while some may reflect the circulation of common descriptions through the radio network.

Several factors illustrate this problem:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Attention was directed to a specific target area.** Officers often knew where to look before observing anything.
* **Descriptions spread rapidly.** Features reported by early witnesses could become reference points for later observers.
* **Night-time conditions increase uncertainty.** Distance, size, speed and altitude are difficult to estimate accurately after dark.
* **Authority effects may matter.** Information coming from fellow officers can carry greater credibility than rumours from the public.

</div>

These concerns do not invalidate the sightings. Instead, they mean that agreement between witnesses should not automatically be treated as proof of independence.

## Why the Radio Evidence Cuts Both Ways

The police radio element is one reason the Lumberton case remains notable within North Carolina UFO history. It provides something more substantial than scattered anecdotes: a network of trained observers communicating during an unfolding event. Yet the same mechanism complicates efforts to measure how many genuinely independent sightings occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://search.worldcat.org/title/The-Lumberton-report-%3A-UFO-activity-in-southern-North-Carolina-April-3-9-1975/oclc/4881106" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: search.worldcat.org">[WorldCat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">search.worldcat.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO activity in southern North Carolina, April 3-9, 1975The Lumberton report: UFO activity in southern North Carolina, April 3-9...</span></span></span>

Without surviving public archives of complete radio transcripts, investigators must largely rely on later summaries and witness recollections. That limitation makes it difficult to determine precisely which descriptions originated with which witnesses and how much information had already circulated before subsequent reports were made.

As a result, the radio traffic can be viewed in two legitimate ways:

* **As supporting evidence**, because it links sightings across multiple locations and helps establish a coherent timeline.
* **As a source of contamination**, because it may have influenced how later witnesses interpreted lights or objects in the sky.

Most balanced assessments of the Lumberton wave recognise both points simultaneously. The radio network appears to have documented a real sequence of reports moving through south-eastern North Carolina, but it also created conditions in which witness perceptions could become partially synchronised.

## What This Means for Assessing the Lumberton Wave

For historians of North Carolina UFO reports, the police radio system is best understood as a mechanism rather than evidence for any particular explanation. It neither proves that an extraordinary object crossed the region nor reduces the entire episode to rumour.

Instead, it explains how the wave developed. Officers were connected through communications infrastructure, reports spread quickly between towns, and multiple observers responded to information received in real time. That process strengthens confidence that the wave was a genuine social and observational event in April 1975.     var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
