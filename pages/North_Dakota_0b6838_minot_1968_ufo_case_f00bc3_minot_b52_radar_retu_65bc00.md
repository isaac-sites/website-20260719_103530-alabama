## Introduction

The most important question in the [Minot 1968]({{ 'minot-1968/' | relative_url }}) case is not whether the B-52 crew believed they saw something unusual. It is what their radar actually recorded. Unlike many UFO reports, the Minot incident left behind a rare technical record: radar-scope photographs taken aboard a Strategic Air Command B-52, radio transcripts between the crew and ground controllers, and later analyses attempting to reconstruct the target's position and movement. These records strongly suggest that the aircraft's radar displayed a genuine, persistent return separate from the bomber itself. What they do not establish is the identity of that target, nor do they conclusively prove the extraordinary speeds and manoeuvres sometimes claimed in later retellings. ## What the radar photographs and radio transcript preserve

The B-52 involved in the Minot events was returning to base during the early hours of 24 October 1968 when ground controllers alerted the crew to an unidentified radar contact. The bomber's radar navigator then acquired a separate target on the aircraft's own radar system and photographed the display. A sequence of radar-scope images survives, making Minot unusual among military UFO cases because later researchers were not forced to rely solely on witness recollections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/radarscopes/radarscope-index.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Radarscope Photographs &#124; The Minot AFB UFO caseScholarly examination of a UFO sighting at Minot Air Force Base on October 24, 1968. The photographs show a distinct radar return appearing apart from the B-52's position marker. According to both the crew's contemporaneous reports and later technical reviews, the contact remained visible through multiple radar sweeps rather than appearing as a single-frame flash or isolated blip. This persistence is one reason many analysts argue that the return cannot simply be dismissed as random electronic noise.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/radarscopes/radarscope-index.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Radarscope Photographs &#124; The Minot AFB UFO caseScholarly examination of a UFO sighting at Minot Air Force Base on October 24, 1968. The radio transcript is equally important. Ground controllers and the crew discussed the contact in real time rather than reconstructing events later from memory. The transcript shows that radar operators and aircrew were attempting to determine the target's location, range and movement while the event was unfolding. That contemporaneous record gives the case greater evidential value than reports based only on retrospective testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/narrative/narrative-full.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">narrative full</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NARRATIVE &#124; The Minot AFB UFO case &#124; 24 OCTOBER 1968RAPCON alerted the B-52 pilots to the location of the UFO high in the northwest, whic...</span></span></span>

What the material clearly demonstrates is that the crew believed they were tracking something on radar. The evidence is considerably weaker when it comes to determining exactly what that "something" was.

## How analysts reconstructed the target's movement

Several later investigators attempted to reconstruct the geometry of the encounter using the radar photographs, aircraft flight path, radar characteristics and transcript timings. Their goal was not merely to verify that a return existed, but to estimate where the target would have been in relation to the bomber.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/poher/cp-part2.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Part 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">General Descriptions of the B-52 Radarscope...Scholarly examination of a UFO sighting at Minot Air Force Base on October 24, 1968. One recurring conclusion is that the radar return appears to have maintained a relatively consistent separation from the aircraft during part of the encounter. Some reconstructions place the contact roughly a few miles from the B-52 while the bomber manoeuvred. If the photographs and timing assumptions are correct, the return was not simply fixed on the ground beneath the aircraft; it behaved as though it occupied a separate position in the airspace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/105405731/Analysis_of_Radar_and_Air_Visual_UFO_Observations_at_Minot_AFB_North_Dakota_USA_by_Claude_Poher" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[academia.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Analysis of Radar and Air Visual UFO Observations at...The intense radar echo of the UFO (OVNI in French), is located one nautic...</span></span></span>

This finding has often been cited by proponents as evidence that the target was a genuine airborne object. However, the reconstruction depends heavily on assumptions about exact photograph timing, radar settings and aircraft position. Small errors in any of those variables can significantly alter the resulting track. Researchers therefore agree more readily on the existence of a radar return than on the precise trajectory attributed to it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/poher/cp-part2.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Part 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">General Descriptions of the B-52 Radarscope...Scholarly examination of a UFO sighting at Minot Air Force Base on October 24, 1968. The strongest claim that can be made from the surviving material is that a coherent target seems to have been displayed repeatedly on the B-52 radar. Claims about dramatic accelerations, intelligent manoeuvring or extreme speed require additional assumptions beyond what the photographs alone can prove.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/radarscopes/radarscope-index.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Radarscope Photographs &#124; The Minot AFB UFO caseScholarly examination of a UFO sighting at Minot Air Force Base on October 24, 1968. ## Why the famous speed claims remain controversial

Many popular accounts of the Minot incident describe a target accelerating at thousands of miles per hour. Those figures generally come from later calculations rather than direct measurements recorded on the radar photographs themselves.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufocasebook.com/minotafbufo1968.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufocasebook.com">[ufocasebook.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufocasebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufocasebook.com.</span></span></span>

The problem is that speed estimates depend on knowing exactly when each photograph was taken and exactly where the aircraft was during each radar sweep. The surviving records do not always provide that level of precision. If a photograph's timing is uncertain by even a short interval, a modest positional change can be interpreted as either ordinary movement or extraordinary acceleration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minotb52ufo.com/poher/cp-part2.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minotb52ufo.com">[minotb52ufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minotb52ufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Part 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">General Descriptions of the B-52 Radarscope...Scholarly examination of a UFO sighting at Minot Air Force Base on October 24, 1968. Inclu...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 24, 1968</span></span></span>

    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
