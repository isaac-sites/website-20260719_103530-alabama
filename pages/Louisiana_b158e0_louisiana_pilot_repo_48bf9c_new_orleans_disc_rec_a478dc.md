## Introduction

Among [Louisiana]({{ 'which-louisiana-ufo-cases-still-resist/' | relative_url }})’s pilot-reported unidentified-object cases, the September 2017 New Orleans disc sighting stands out because the witness was an experienced aviator on approach to a major airport. Yet it is also one of the state’s weaker aviation mysteries. The report describes a silver, disc-shaped object seen near an aircraft at roughly 1,500 feet, but later examination revealed uncertainty over a basic question: which aircraft actually made the report. Competing references to a Jetstream turboprop and a Pilatus PC-12 appear in different records and retellings, creating a chain-of-custody problem for the evidence itself. Rather than strengthening the case, the conflicting aircraft identification makes it harder to reconstruct exactly what happened and to test possible explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

## The Silver Object Reported Near the Arrival Path

The sighting entered public discussion through Federal Aviation Administration incident records later examined by researchers and journalists studying reports of unidentified aircraft and suspected drones. According to the available summaries, a pilot approaching New Orleans reported a silver object described as disc-shaped near the aircraft’s flight path at an altitude of around 1,500 feet. The object was reportedly seen only briefly, with no photograph, radar track or independent sensor evidence released publicly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

What made the report unusual was the shape description. Many aviation sightings involve lights, distant objects or possible drones. A metallic “disc” is a rarer description in FAA records. The War Zone’s review of thousands of FAA reports highlighted the New Orleans incident as one of a small number involving objects described with terms such as “disc” or “cylinder”, rather than conventional drone shapes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

At the same time, the available information is limited. Publicly released summaries do not establish the object’s size, speed or precise distance from the aircraft. Those details matter because a small nearby object can appear similar to a larger object farther away, especially during approach and descent when pilots are managing multiple visual references and changing angles of view. Without range information, the observation remains a description rather than a measurement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

## Why the Jetstream and PC-12 Records Conflict

The central problem with the New Orleans case is not the description of the object but the uncertainty surrounding the reporting aircraft.

As the incident circulated through UFO and aviation discussions, different versions identified the witness aircraft differently. Some references associated the sighting with a Jetstream turboprop, while others pointed to a Pilatus PC-12. Both are legitimate aircraft types, but they differ significantly in configuration, operating profile and operator base. If investigators cannot confidently determine which aircraft was involved, reconstructing the encounter becomes much more difficult.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

This matters for several reasons:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* **Flight-path reconstruction becomes uncertain.** Different aircraft records would imply different routes, speeds and positions.
* **Crew verification becomes difficult.** Identifying the correct crew is essential if follow-up interviews are ever conducted.
* **Radar and traffic analysis become less reliable.** Investigators attempting to match the report to air-traffic data need the correct aircraft identity.
* **Alternative explanations become harder to test.** Potential explanations such as another aircraft, a drone, a balloon or a visual illusion depend on the exact geometry of the encounter.

</div>

The conflict appears to stem from later reporting and database interpretation rather than from any evidence that two separate aircraft independently observed the same object. Publicly available material does not resolve the discrepancy. As a result, researchers are left with a sighting report whose most basic identifying details remain unsettled.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

## What the Report Proves and What Remains Unresolved

The strongest conclusion supported by the evidence is modest: a pilot reported seeing something unusual near New Orleans and considered it sufficiently noteworthy to enter the aviation reporting system. That fact alone gives the case more credibility than anonymous internet stories or rumours lacking documentation. FAA reporting mechanisms exist precisely because unexpected aerial objects can present safety concerns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/41573/the-strangest-encounters-with-unidentified-aircraft-weve-found-in-the-faas-huge-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Strangest Encounters With Unidentified Aircraft We&#x27;ve...20 Jul 2021 — We found over 50 highly unusual incidents with our new FAA...</span></span></span>

However, several important questions remain unanswered:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Was the object actually disc-shaped, or did it merely appear that way from the pilot’s viewing angle?
* How far away was it?
* Was it stationary, moving independently or only appearing to move because of relative motion between observer and object?
* Was it a drone, balloon, another aircraft, or something more mundane?
* Which aircraft and crew made the original observation?

</div>

None of these questions can be answered confidently from the public record.
