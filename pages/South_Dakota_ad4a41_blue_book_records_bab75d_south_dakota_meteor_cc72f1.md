## Introduction

Project [Blue Book]({{ 'blue-book-eb3f6b/' | relative_url }}) attributed several [South Dakota]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-south-dakotas/' | relative_url }}) UFO reports to meteors, especially during the busy reporting period of 1965. On paper, those explanations often appear straightforward: a brilliant light crossed the sky, witnesses saw a brief flash, and investigators concluded that a meteor had been responsible. Yet a closer look at the surviving records shows that some of these decisions were less definitive than the official classifications suggest. In at least two well-known South Dakota cases from February and March 1965, witnesses questioned whether a meteor could account for what they had observed, while the surviving Blue Book documentation offered only limited justification for its conclusions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society. All Rights Reserved.September 13, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 13, 2012</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/South_Dakota_ad4a41_blue_book_records_bab75d_south_dakota_meteor_cc72f1-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Meteor Disputes illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/South_Dakota_ad4a41_blue_book_records_bab75d_south_dakota_meteor_cc72f1-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/South_Dakota_ad4a41_blue_book_records_bab75d_south_dakota_meteor_cc72f1-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

This does not mean the meteor explanations were necessarily wrong. Rather, it illustrates a recurring feature of Project Blue Book: investigators often preferred a plausible conventional explanation once one appeared available, even if the surviving file did not fully address every aspect of the witness testimony.

## The February and March 1965 reports

The strongest examples come from two reports investigated within weeks of one another during 1965, a year that saw a sharp rise in UFO reports across the United States.

On the night of **15–16 February 1965**, several airline crews flying across South Dakota reported an exceptionally bright blue light that illuminated their flight decks. Blue Book concluded that the object was a meteor. A bright fireball could indeed produce a sudden flash visible over a wide region, and such events are well documented in astronomical observation. The surviving summary, however, provides little detail explaining why investigators ruled out other possibilities or how they reconciled the accounts from multiple professional aviation witnesses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society. All Rights Reserved.September 13, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 13, 2012</span></span></span>

A second case followed on **30 March 1965**, when a witness near Watertown reported a bright green object crossing the sky. Again, Project Blue Book classified the sighting as a meteor. According to historian Lawrence H. Larsen's examination of the surviving files, that conclusion "ran directly counter" to the witness's own interpretation of what had been seen. The surviving record gives little indication that investigators attempted to explain precisely why the witness believed the object behaved differently from a typical meteor.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society. All Rights Reserved.September 13, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 13, 2012</span></span></span>

Neither case contains evidence that definitively disproves the meteor explanation. Instead, both demonstrate that the official classification sometimes rested on a concise judgement rather than a detailed reconstruction of the event.

## Why Blue Book favoured meteor explanations

Meteor identifications were common throughout Project Blue Book, not only in South Dakota but across the United States. By the mid-1960s, investigators routinely considered astronomical causes whenever reports involved:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* brilliant flashes;
* fast-moving lights;
* green or blue colours;
* short observation times; or
* objects leaving no lasting physical evidence.

</div>

These criteria are reasonable because exceptionally bright meteors, known as fireballs, can produce exactly those characteristics. They may briefly illuminate landscapes or aircraft, appear green or blue because of their composition or atmospheric effects, and disappear within seconds. Project Blue Book's own guidance grouped meteors with other recognised astronomical explanations such as planets, stars and comets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://prologue.blogs.archives.gov/2018/04/16/ufos-natural-explanations/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prologue.blogs.archives.gov">[Pieces of History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prologue.blogs.archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pieces of History UFOs: Natural Explanations – Pieces of History</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pieces of History UFOs: Natural Explanations – Pieces of History</span></span></span>

Operational pressures also encouraged this approach. During periods of increased reporting, investigators often worked from brief military messages, witness summaries and astronomical information rather than conducting lengthy field investigations. If a meteor was known or considered likely, it became an efficient explanation that allowed the case to be closed.

## Where witness accounts challenged the official verdicts

The South Dakota meteor cases are notable because the disagreement was not simply between believers and sceptics. Instead, the tension lay between official conclusions and the details preserved in the witness accounts.

Several recurring issues appear in these cases.

**Witnesses described behaviour they believed exceeded a meteor's normal appearance.** In the March 1965 Watertown report, the witness evidently felt that the object's characteristics did not match an ordinary meteor, although the surviving summary provides only a limited account of those objections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society. All Rights Reserved.September 13, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 13, 2012</span></span></span>

**Professional observers were not automatically persuasive.** The February case involved airline crews whose experience observing the night sky might ordinarily strengthen the evidential value of their reports. Blue Book nevertheless concluded that the phenomenon was a meteor without leaving a detailed explanation of why trained observers had apparently misidentified it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society. All Rights Reserved.September 13, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 13, 2012</span></span></span>

**The surviving documentation is incomplete.** Many Blue Book files now consist of record cards, summary sheets or short correspondence rather than comprehensive investigative reports. As a result, it is often impossible to determine whether investigators considered evidence that no longer survives or whether the investigation itself was relatively limited.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society. ## How convincing were the meteor explanations?

Taken individually, both South Dakota cases remain compatible with genuine meteor events. Bright fireballs can be spectacular, widely visible and sufficiently unusual that experienced observers mistake them for something unfamiliar. From a purely astronomical standpoint, the official explanation is plausible.

The difficulty lies in the quality of the surviving evidence rather than the physics of meteors themselves.

The Blue Book files generally do not show investigators systematically addressing every point raised by witnesses before assigning the meteor classification. Instead, the records often present the explanation as a final administrative judgement. For historians, that makes it difficult to distinguish between a carefully supported identification and one based largely on probability.

This pattern fits the broader picture of Project Blue Book in South Dakota. Most reports ultimately received conventional explanations, but the level of documented reasoning varied considerably from case to case. Consequently, the meteor classifications should usually be understood as the Air Force's best official assessment rather than as conclusive demonstrations that every witness observation had been fully explained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sdhspress.com/journal/south-dakota-history-12-1/united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos-great-plains-encounters/vol-12-no-1-united-states-air-force-efforts-to-investigate-ufos.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sdhspress.com">[South Dakota Historical Society Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sdhspress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">vol 12 no 1 united states air force efforts to investigate ufos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">South Dakota Historical Society PressCopyright © 1982 by the South Dakota State Historical Society.     if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
