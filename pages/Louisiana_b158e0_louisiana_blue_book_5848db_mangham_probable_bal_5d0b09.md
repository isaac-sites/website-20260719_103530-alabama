## Introduction

The Mangham sighting of May 1960 is one of the most revealing cases in [Louisiana]({{ 'which-louisiana-ufo-cases-still-resist/' | relative_url }})’s Project Blue Book records because it combines dramatic eyewitness testimony, military interest and a surprisingly ordinary official conclusion. A bright object observed near Mangham in northeastern Louisiana reportedly remained visible for almost two hours and attracted enough attention that interceptor aircraft were dispatched to investigate. ## What Witnesses Reported Over Mangham

According to the Project Blue Book file, the sighting occurred in May 1960 near Mangham, a small town in Richland Parish. Witnesses described a bright aerial object that remained visible for an unusually long period. One observer was identified as both a sheriff and a licensed civilian pilot, a detail that gave the report additional weight within the Air Force investigation. The duration of the observation and the inability of witnesses to identify the object immediately made it noteworthy within Blue Book procedures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. The object's reported persistence was particularly significant. Many Blue Book reports involved fleeting lights or brief observations, but the Mangham object was said to remain in view for close to two hours. Because the sighting appeared unusual enough to warrant concern, military authorities treated it as more than a routine civilian report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. One of the most frequently repeated details is that interceptor aircraft were launched from England Air Force Base in Louisiana to investigate. The pilots were unable to make a positive interception or identification. To later readers, the mere fact that military aircraft were sent aloft can sound like evidence that officials believed they were dealing with something extraordinary. The file itself suggests a more cautious interpretation: the launch demonstrates that the report was taken seriously enough to check, not that the Air Force concluded an unknown craft was present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. ## Why the Air Force Favoured a Balloon

The most important evidence in the case came from the subsequent investigation rather than the original sighting. Blue Book personnel reviewed weather information and consulted Air Force weather specialists. According to the surviving report, the weather office at England Air Force Base stated definitively that the object was a weather balloon released from Natchez, Mississippi.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. That conclusion may seem unsatisfying until the practical circumstances are considered. Weather balloons are designed to be highly visible. Under favourable lighting conditions they can appear bright, reflective and stationary even when drifting considerable distances. Their apparent motion can be difficult to judge, especially when viewed from the ground without reference points. A balloon at altitude may seem to hover, climb, descend or change direction because of shifting winds at different atmospheric levels.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[wikimedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. The timing also mattered. Investigators were not merely speculating that a balloon might have been present. The file records a specific candidate source: a weather balloon reportedly launched from Natchez. The Air Force regarded the correspondence between the sighting and known balloon activity as strong enough to support a probable identification. Although the interceptors failed to obtain a direct confirmation, Blue Book did not require absolute proof in every case. Investigators frequently assigned a “probable” explanation when available evidence pointed more strongly toward a conventional object than toward an unknown phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. LOUISIANA, a lic~naed civilian...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1960</span></span></span>

The Mangham file therefore demonstrates an important feature of Blue Book methodology. Witness credibility alone did not determine the outcome. Investigators could regard observers as sincere and competent while still concluding that they had misidentified a conventional object. ## Why Later Archive Labels Caused Confusion

The Mangham incident has generated occasional confusion among UFO researchers because different summaries and catalogue systems do not always emphasise the same part of the record. The full Blue Book documentation paints a more nuanced picture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. Part of the problem is that Project Blue Book records passed through several stages of reproduction and indexing after the programme ended. Microfilm copies, archive databases and secondary catalogues sometimes compress complicated investigations into a few words. A brief label cannot easily capture the distinction between an object that was initially unidentified and one that investigators later judged probably explained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/details/ProjectBlueBookIndexes" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book Indexes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet ArchiveProject Blue Book Indexes, 1947-1969: United States Air...30 Apr 2023 — Indexes for UFO sightings recorded by Project B...</span></span></span>

## What the Mangham Case Reveals About Louisiana's Blue Book Files

Within the broader Louisiana record, Mangham stands as a case where the investigation itself is more instructive than the original sighting. The report demonstrates that Air Force personnel could devote substantial attention to an incident, consult technical specialists and still arrive at a conventional explanation. It also shows why modern readers should distinguish between the excitement generated by a sighting and the conclusion reached after review.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released. The incident remains notable because it demonstrates how an apparently remarkable report could evolve, through investigation, into one of the clearest examples of a likely balloon identification in the state's Blue Book archive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Project_Blue_Book_report_-_1960-05-7812632-Mangham-Louisiana.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: upload.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">upload.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Blue Book report 1960 05 7812632 Mangham Louisiana</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The investigation was made on 6 and 7 June 1960 … the object sighted was a weather balloon released.     var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
