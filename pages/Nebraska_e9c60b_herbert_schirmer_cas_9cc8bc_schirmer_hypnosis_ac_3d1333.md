## Introduction

One of the most important questions in the Herbert [Schirmer case]({{ 'schirmer-case/' | relative_url }}) is not whether he reported seeing something unusual near Ashland, [Nebraska]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-nebraskas-ufo/' | relative_url }}), in December 1967. It is how his account changed after hypnosis. Schirmer's original police report described a strange object near a highway junction and an unsettling encounter that he could not fully explain. The now-famous elements of the story—humanoid occupants, a journey aboard a craft, telepathic communication, and messages about the visitors' origins—appeared later during hypnotic sessions conducted by psychologist R. Leo Sprinkle as part of the investigation connected with the University of Colorado's UFO study.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/WorldHerald/posts/patrolman-herb-schirmer-scrawled-a-short-message-summarizing-the-nights-bizarre-/10157203186119630/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">At 2:30 a.m</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">At 2:30 a.m. December 3, 1967, I saw a UFO at the...On February 13, 1968, Schirmer is hypnotized in Boulder, Colorado, by R. For supporters of the case, hypnosis helped recover memories hidden by trauma or by an altered state of consciousness. For sceptics, the same process illustrates how suggestive interviewing can expand a limited recollection into a much more elaborate narrative. Understanding that shift is essential to understanding why the Schirmer case remains one of Nebraska's best-known UFO incidents.

## What Schirmer Reported Before Hypnosis

The key point is that Schirmer's earliest account was much narrower than the version that later entered UFO literature.

Immediately after the incident, he recorded that he had seen a flying saucer-like object while on patrol. He described unusual lights, an object near the roadway, and a sense that something extraordinary had occurred. Contemporary discussions of the case note that his initial report focused on the sighting itself rather than on direct contact with extraterrestrial beings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/WorldHerald/posts/patrolman-herb-schirmer-scrawled-a-short-message-summarizing-the-nights-bizarre-/10157203186119630/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">At 2:30 a.m</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">At 2:30 a.m. December 3, 1967, I saw a UFO at the...On February 13, 1968, Schirmer is hypnotized in Boulder, Colorado, by R. What is largely absent from the earliest record are the details that later made the case famous:

## How Suggestive Questioning Introduced New Details

In February 1968, Schirmer underwent hypnosis with psychologist R. Leo Sprinkle, who was consulting on the UFO investigation. During these sessions, the story expanded dramatically.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/WorldHerald/posts/patrolman-herb-schirmer-scrawled-a-short-message-summarizing-the-nights-bizarre-/10157203186119630/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">At 2:30 a.m</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">At 2:30 a.m. December 3, 1967, I saw a UFO at the...On February 13, 1968, Schirmer is hypnotized in Boulder, Colorado, by R. Under hypnosis, Schirmer recalled events that he had not reported initially. According to later summaries of the sessions, he described humanoid occupants emerging from the object, being unable to draw his weapon because of a mental influence, entering the craft, and receiving information through telepathic communication. He also recounted details about the occupants' appearance, symbols on their uniforms, and explanations allegedly provided about their activities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nalda.ch/products/9786130457662" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nalda.ch">[nalda.ch]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nalda.ch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Schirmer Abduction Lambert M. ## Why Investigators Treated the New Memories Differently

Even among people interested in UFO reports, hypnosis has long been controversial.

By the late twentieth century, many psychologists and memory researchers argued that hypnosis can increase confidence in memories without increasing their accuracy. A person may sincerely experience a recalled scene as real while unknowingly filling gaps with imagination, expectations, or cues from the interviewer.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspectives_on_the_alien_abduction_phenomenon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Perspectives on the alien abduction phenomenon</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Perspectives on the alien abduction phenomenon</span></span></span>

This concern is especially relevant in the Schirmer case because the most extraordinary parts of the story emerged only after hypnosis. Critics point out that the process did not merely recover a few missing details. It produced an entirely new layer of narrative involving beings, conversations, technology, and motivations that were not present in the original report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://groups.google.com/g/de.alt.ufo/c/640DXzGY91Q" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: groups.google.com">[Google Groups]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">groups.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">640DXz GY91Q</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">640DXz GY91Q</span></span></span>

Sceptical researchers have argued that alien-abduction stories often become more detailed during hypnotic recall and that investigators can unintentionally influence the direction of those memories. Studies of hypnosis and false-memory formation are frequently cited in discussions of such cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrative_of_the_abduction_phenomenon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Narrative of the abduction phenomenon</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Narrative of the abduction phenomenon</span></span></span>

Supporters of Schirmer's account respond that hypnosis revealed memories that had been suppressed or forgotten due to the unusual nature of the encounter. ## Why Vivid Hypnotic Memories Are Not Reliable Verification

The central lesson from the Schirmer case is that vividness is not the same as evidence.

A detailed story can feel compelling because it contains specific descriptions, dialogue, symbols, and sequences of events. Yet those qualities do not independently verify that the events occurred. The strongest evidence for any historical claim comes from contemporary records, physical traces, independent witnesses, photographs, instrument data, or corroborating documentation.

In Schirmer's case, the detailed alien-contact narrative rests primarily on memories reported during hypnosis. The additional information was not independently confirmed by physical evidence, and the dramatic features of the account appeared only after the hypnotic sessions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/WorldHerald/posts/patrolman-herb-schirmer-scrawled-a-short-message-summarizing-the-nights-bizarre-/10157203186119630/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">At 2:30 a.m</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">At 2:30 a.m. December 3, 1967, I saw a UFO at the...On February 13, 1968, Schirmer is hypnotized in Boulder, Colorado, by R.     var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
