## Introduction

Did a UFO kill the horse called Snippy? Based on the evidence available, the answer is that there is no reliable proof that a UFO had anything to do with the animal’s death. Yet the 1967 case remains one of the most influential stories in [Colorado]({{ 'what-colorados-ufo-history-really/' | relative_url }}) UFO history because it helped transform the [San Luis Valley]({{ 'san-luis-valley/' | relative_url }}) from a region with scattered sighting reports into a nationally recognised UFO hotspot. What made the incident memorable was not just the death of a horse near Alamosa, but the combination of unusual physical details, widespread media attention and later claims that extraterrestrials had somehow been involved. Subsequent investigation, however, found that the extraordinary interpretation rested on weak foundations and that natural explanations could account for much of what witnesses described.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.denverlibrary.org/news/western-history/ufos-and-horse-called-snippy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.denverlibrary.org">[Denver Public Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.denverlibrary.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Denver Public LibraryUFOs and a Horse Called Snippy20 Oct 2020 — The horse and cattle mutilations were distressing, to say the least...</span></span></span>

## What Was Reported Near Alamosa in 1967?

In September 1967, a three-year-old Appaloosa mare was found dead on ranchland near Alamosa in Colorado’s San Luis Valley. Although the case became famous as “[Snippy]({{ 'snippy/' | relative_url }})”, later accounts noted that the dead mare was actually named Lady; Snippy was the name of her sire. The misidentification nevertheless became permanent in popular culture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

The discovery quickly attracted attention because witnesses reported several unusual features. Flesh appeared to be missing from parts of the head and neck, there was said to be little or no visible blood around the carcass, and observers described the tissue removal as remarkably clean. Additional claims soon followed, including reports of odd odours, dark patches on the ground, unusual colour changes in exposed bone and possible landing traces nearby.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.denverlibrary.org/news/western-history/ufos-and-horse-called-snippy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.denverlibrary.org">[Denver Public Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.denverlibrary.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Denver Public LibraryUFOs and a Horse Called Snippy20 Oct 2020 — The horse and cattle mutilations were distressing, to say the least...</span></span></span>

These details emerged at a time when public interest in flying saucers was already high. Newspapers across the United States ran dramatic headlines linking the horse’s death to UFOs. The story spread far beyond Colorado and became one of the first widely publicised incidents in which an unexplained animal death was connected to extraterrestrial speculation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

## How the UFO Explanation Took Hold

The UFO interpretation did not emerge from physical evidence alone. It developed because the horse’s death occurred within a broader atmosphere of local UFO interest. Reports of strange lights in the San Luis Valley had circulated for years, and some witnesses connected those stories to the unusual condition of the carcass.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

Several reported details seemed difficult to explain at first glance:

* Claims that flesh had been removed with surgical precision.
* Assertions that there was no blood at the scene.
* Reports of strange smells and discolouration.
* Alleged landing marks or scorched areas near the carcass.
* Accounts of unusual aerial objects seen in the region around the same period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.denverlibrary.org/news/western-history/ufos-and-horse-called-snippy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.denverlibrary.org">[Denver Public Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.denverlibrary.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Denver Public LibraryUFOs and a Horse Called Snippy20 Oct 2020 — The horse and cattle mutilations were distressing, to say the least...</span></span></span>

Once national newspapers began presenting the incident as a possible flying-saucer mystery, the story acquired a life of its own. UFO writers and paranormal researchers later revisited the case, often treating it as a foundational example of the animal mutilation phenomenon that became prominent during the 1970s. In popular UFO literature, Snippy was increasingly portrayed as evidence of extraterrestrial experimentation despite the absence of direct proof linking the death to any aerial object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

The case therefore became important less because of what investigators could demonstrate and more because of its cultural influence. It provided a dramatic narrative that connected UFO sightings, livestock deaths and the isolated landscape of the San Luis Valley into a single enduring legend.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.denverlibrary.org/news/western-history/ufos-and-horse-called-snippy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.denverlibrary.org">[Denver Public Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.denverlibrary.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Denver Public LibraryUFOs and a Horse Called Snippy20 Oct 2020 — The horse and cattle mutilations were distressing, to say the least...</span></span></span>

## What Later Investigation Found

As the story spread, investigators examined whether the extraordinary claims could withstand scrutiny. The most important review came from researchers associated with the University of Colorado UFO study, commonly known as the Condon Committee. Their conclusion was straightforward: they found no evidence that the horse’s death was associated with any abnormal or extraordinary cause.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

This conclusion mattered because many of the case’s most dramatic features turned out to be less clear-cut than newspaper reports suggested. Observations were made after the carcass had already been exposed to the environment, and many claims relied on recollections rather than controlled documentation. Some details changed as the story was retold, making it difficult to determine which observations were original and which emerged later through repetition.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

Investigators and sceptical researchers also noted that scavengers and natural decomposition can create effects that appear surprisingly precise. Dry conditions, insect activity and animal feeding patterns may remove soft tissue while leaving other parts relatively intact. Likewise, apparent absences of blood around a carcass are not unusual once decomposition advances and body fluids disperse into soil or are consumed by scavengers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

Some sensational claims weakened further under examination. Reports of radiation anomalies, landing traces and other physical evidence were never supported by conclusive documentation capable of demonstrating an extraordinary event. The case remained unusual, but unusual is not the same as unexplained by natural processes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutilation_of_%22Snippy%22_the_horse" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mutilation of &quot;Snippy&quot; the horse</span></span></span>

## Why the Case Still Matters in Colorado UFO History

Even though the UFO explanation lacks convincing evidence, the Snippy incident occupies a central place in Colorado UFO folklore. It helped establish the San Luis Valley as a destination for UFO enthusiasts and linked the region's reputation to later reports of cattle mutilations, mysterious lights and paranormal claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.denverlibrary.org/news/western-history/ufos-and-horse-called-snippy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.denverlibrary.org">[Denver Public Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.denverlibrary.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Denver Public LibraryUFOs and a Horse Called Snippy20 Oct 2020 — The horse and cattle mutilations were distressing, to say the least...</span></span></span>
