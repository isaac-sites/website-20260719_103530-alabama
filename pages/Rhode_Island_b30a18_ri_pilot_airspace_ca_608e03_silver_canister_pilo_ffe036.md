## Introduction

The [Rhode Island]({{ 'what-really-defines-rhode-islands-ufo/' | relative_url }}) “silver canister” encounter attracted attention because it came from a pilot speaking directly to air traffic control rather than from a second-hand story. In the reported incident, publicised in late 2025 after aviation audio circulated online, a pilot flying at roughly 3,500 feet near Rhode Island described a small silver cylindrical object close to the aircraft's wingtip. The sighting occurred in controlled airspace and was reported in real time, giving the case more credibility than many anonymous UFO claims. Yet the central fact about the event is also the reason it remains unresolved: there is very little evidence beyond the pilot’s own observation and the recorded radio exchange.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. ## What the Pilot and Controller Actually Reported

The most important evidence is the recorded conversation itself. According to reports based on the air traffic control audio, the pilot described a “small silver canister” that appeared to be near the aircraft while the plane was flying at approximately 3,500 feet. The pilot stated that the object seemed to be “standing still” and was located off the wingtip. When asked whether it might be attached to anything, the pilot reportedly replied that it did not appear to be tethered and seemed to be hovering on its own.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. Small silver canister,” said the pilot, flying a Piper PA...</span></span></span>

Reports identified the aircraft as a Piper PA-32RT-300T Turbo Lance II. The controller did not provide an identification for the object and there is no public indication that air traffic control had a corresponding radar target associated with the sighting. The exchange ended with a light-hearted remark from the controller that later became widely quoted in media coverage: “Good luck with the aliens.”<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[nypost.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. Small silver canister,” said the pilot, flying a Piper PA...</span></span></span>

The value of this evidence lies in its immediacy. The pilot was not recounting a memory months or years later. The observation was reported during flight operations, reducing some of the distortion that can occur in later retellings. At the same time, the recording documents only what the witness believed he saw. It does not independently verify the object's size, distance, speed, or physical nature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. ## Which Details Were Amplified or Muddled in Coverage

As the story spread through news sites, social media posts and video channels, several details became less clear.

One common misunderstanding is the distance between the aircraft and the object. Some retellings implied that the object was only a few feet away. The publicly reported audio does not provide a measured separation. The pilot described the object as being near the wingtip, but estimating distance to a small object in open sky is notoriously difficult, especially during a brief encounter. Without range data, nobody can confidently determine whether the object was a few feet away, hundreds of feet away, or farther still.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. Small silver canister,” said the pilot, flying a Piper PA...</span></span></span>

Another point that became exaggerated is the idea that the object was definitely hovering. The pilot reported that it appeared stationary relative to the aircraft. In aviation, however, an object can seem motionless because of geometry and relative motion. Balloons, distant aircraft, drones, birds and even airborne debris can sometimes appear fixed in space for short periods depending on viewing angle and closing speed. The pilot’s description is genuine evidence of perception, but not direct proof of actual hovering.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. Small silver canister,” said the pilot, flying a Piper PA...</span></span></span>

Coverage also sometimes transformed the event into a confirmed “UFO beside a jet”. In reality, the reports describe a private aircraft rather than an airline jet, and the available information does not establish the object's identity, origin or behaviour beyond the brief visual observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. ## Why the Evidence Remains Thin

For a pilot encounter to become a strong aviation UFO case, investigators normally look for multiple forms of corroboration. These can include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* Radar detections.
* Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast (ADS-B) data.
* Cockpit photographs or video.
* Ground witnesses.
* Additional pilot reports.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[nypost.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. Small silver canister,” said the pilot, flying a Piper PA...</span></span></span>
* Physical recovery of an object.
* Official investigative findings.

</div>

None of those have emerged publicly in connection with the Rhode Island silver-canister report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2025/12/21/us-news/pilot-reports-silver-cannister-ufo-to-air-traffic-control-told-good-luck-with-the-aliens/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It was right off our wingtip. Small silver canister,” said the pilot, flying a Piper PA...</span></span></span>

This absence matters because human observers are very good at noticing unusual objects but much less reliable at judging size, distance and speed without reference points.
