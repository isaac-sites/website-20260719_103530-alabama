## Introduction

The bright spiral that appeared over Maunakea in January 2023 is one of the clearest examples of how a dramatic sky event can briefly look like a UFO while ultimately having a well-supported conventional explanation. A glowing, rotating pattern was captured by cameras near the summit observatories on Maunakea, prompting widespread speculation online. Yet within days, astronomers and spaceflight trackers linked the phenomenon to the upper stage of a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket that had launched a military GPS satellite from Florida earlier that day.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://subarutelescope.org/en/news/topics/2023/02/01/3225.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: subarutelescope.org">[subarutelescope.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">subarutelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The spiral seems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Flying Spiral over Maunakea &#124; Topics &amp; Announcements1 Feb 2023 — In the early morning of January 18, 2023, Subaru-Asahi Star Camera cap...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2023</span></span></span>

The spiral was real, visually striking and unusual. The key question was not whether people saw something, but what they were actually seeing.

## Was the Maunakea Spiral Really a UFO?

In the early hours of 18 January 2023, the Subaru-Asahi Star Camera on Maunakea recorded a bright white orb that expanded into a large spiral shape before gradually fading from view. The footage quickly spread across social media and news outlets because the structure looked unlike aircraft lights, meteors or ordinary astronomical objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://subarutelescope.org/en/news/topics/2023/02/01/3225.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: subarutelescope.org">[subarutelescope.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">subarutelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The spiral seems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Flying Spiral over Maunakea &#124; Topics &amp; Announcements1 Feb 2023 — In the early morning of January 18, 2023, Subaru-Asahi Star Camera cap...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2023</span></span></span>

The appearance was especially dramatic because it was observed from one of the world's premier astronomical sites. Maunakea's high altitude, dark skies and exceptionally clear atmosphere allowed the camera to record details that might have been invisible from more light-polluted locations. Researchers at the Subaru Telescope noted that the spiral was probably bright enough to be visible to the naked eye, but the sensitive camera captured its structure much more clearly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://subarutelescope.org/en/news/topics/2023/02/01/3225.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: subarutelescope.org">[subarutelescope.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">subarutelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The spiral seems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Flying Spiral over Maunakea &#124; Topics &amp; Announcements1 Feb 2023 — In the early morning of January 18, 2023, Subaru-Asahi Star Camera cap...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2023</span></span></span>

At first glance, the event seemed mysterious. It did not resemble a conventional aircraft, appeared high above the horizon and displayed a geometric shape that many viewers associated with science-fiction imagery rather than familiar aerospace activity. Those characteristics made it exactly the kind of sighting that can generate UFO reports.

## What the Summit Camera Recorded

The camera footage showed a sequence rather than a single flash. A compact bright point appeared first, then broadened into a glowing cloud. As the cloud expanded, it developed a rotating spiral pattern that drifted across the sky before dispersing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/science/2023/jan/31/bizarre-whirlpool-hawaii-spacex-florida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">bizarre whirlpool hawaii spacex florida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianBizarre &#x27;whirlpool&#x27; appears in night sky above Hawaii &#124; Space31 Jan 2023 — Researchers believe the strange phenomenon is link...</span></span></span>

An important detail is that the spiral was not observed solely by one witness. It was captured by an automated astronomical camera operating continuously at the summit. Researchers were alerted to the phenomenon by viewers watching the observatory livestream, after which telescope staff reviewed the recordings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/d0f19d48010a2ccc26ccba31387d47d5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The images, taken by a camera near the Subaru telescope, showed a white orb evolving into a spiral before fading away. ## How the Falcon 9 Flight Matched the Sighting

The strongest evidence against a UFO interpretation came from the timing and location of a SpaceX launch.

On 18 January 2023, a Falcon 9 rocket launched a US military GPS satellite from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida. Analysts quickly noted that the expected position of the Falcon 9 upper stage after orbital operations corresponded closely with the location of the spiral seen from Hawaii.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://phys.org/news/2023-01-camera-captures-night-sky-spiral.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: phys.org">[phys.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">phys.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Camera captures night sky spiral after Space X rocket launch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">18 by a camera at the summit of Mauna Kea outside the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan&#x27;s Subaru...Read more...</span></span></span>

The National Astronomical Observatory of Japan, which operates the Subaru Telescope, stated that discussions among observers and spaceflight enthusiasts rapidly connected the sighting to satellite deployment operations associated with the SpaceX mission. The observatory subsequently described the spiral as being related to the launch.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://subarutelescope.org/en/news/topics/2023/02/01/3225.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: subarutelescope.org">[subarutelescope.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">subarutelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The spiral seems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Flying Spiral over Maunakea &#124; Topics &amp; Announcements1 Feb 2023 — In the early morning of January 18, 2023, Subaru-Asahi Star Camera cap...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2023</span></span></span>

Independent reporting reached the same conclusion. News organisations covering the event noted that researchers believed the phenomenon was produced by the Falcon 9 second stage after completing its primary mission. The coincidence in timing was not vague or approximate; the rocket had launched only hours earlier, and the observed sky position matched where the upper stage was expected to be operating.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://phys.org/news/2023-01-camera-captures-night-sky-spiral.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: phys.org">[phys.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">phys.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Camera captures night sky spiral after Space X rocket launch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">18 by a camera at the summit of Mauna Kea outside the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan&#x27;s Subaru...Read more...</span></span></span>

Unlike many UFO cases, there is little competing evidence pointing to an alternative explanation.

## Why Rocket Plumes Can Form Glowing Spirals

The explanation sounds strange until the physics are understood.

After delivering a payload, a rocket's upper stage may vent excess propellant or perform manoeuvres before re-entering the atmosphere. If gas is released while the stage is rotating, the escaping material spreads outward in a corkscrew pattern. Sunlight can illuminate the expanding cloud even when observers on the ground are in darkness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2023/01/24/flying-spiral-spotted-over-mauna-kea/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hawaiinewsnow.com">[hawaiinewsnow.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hawaiinewsnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hawaiinewsnow.com.</span></span></span>

Several factors combine to make the effect spectacular:

* **High altitude:** The gas is released far above the atmosphere where it can expand into a huge cloud.
* **Rocket rotation:** A spinning upper stage turns the expanding plume into a spiral.
* **Sunlight illumination:** The cloud remains sunlit long after sunset or before sunrise at ground level.
* **Dark sky background:** Against a dark sky, the illuminated plume appears exceptionally bright and well defined.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.universetoday.com/articles/aurora-hunter-todd-salat-captures-a-bizarre-spiral-made-by-a-spacex-fuel-dump" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: universetoday.com">[universetoday.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">universetoday.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aurora hunter todd salat captures a bizarre spiral made by a spacex fuel dump</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">aurora hunter todd salat captures a bizarre spiral made by a spacex fuel dump</span></span></span>

## What the Incident Reveals About Hawaii UFO Reports


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

The Maunakea spiral remains a useful case study within Hawaii's broader UFO history because it shows how a genuine and unusual visual phenomenon can be mistaken for something unknown.

</div>

Witnesses were not imagining the object. The spiral existed, was recorded by professional equipment and looked extraordinary. Yet the best available evidence points to a human-made source rather than an unexplained craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://subarutelescope.org/en/news/topics/2023/02/01/3225.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: subarutelescope.org">[subarutelescope.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">subarutelescope.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The spiral seems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Flying Spiral over Maunakea &#124; Topics &amp; Announcements1 Feb 2023 — In the early morning of January 18, 2023, Subaru-Asahi Star Camera cap...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2023</span></span></span>

The incident also highlights a recurring feature of Hawaiian sightings: the islands' clear skies allow observers to see distant aerospace events that would go unnoticed in many other places. Rocket exhaust, satellite operations and other high-altitude phenomena can remain visible for long periods and appear much closer than they really are. That combination of excellent visibility and limited distance cues helps explain why some of Hawaii's most striking UFO reports later receive conventional explanations.

In the case of the Maunakea spiral, later investigation did not weaken the rocket explanation. Instead, the accumulating evidence—from observatory staff, spaceflight tracking and comparisons with similar events—made the SpaceX connection increasingly convincing.     if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
