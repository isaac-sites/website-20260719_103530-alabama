## Introduction

The most famous detail in the [Mantell incident]({{ 'mantell-incident/' | relative_url }}) is also one of its least secure. For decades, books and documentaries have repeated the claim that Captain Thomas Mantell radioed that he was pursuing “a metallic object of tremendous size” before his fatal crash over [Kentucky]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-kentuckys-ufo/' | relative_url }}) on 7 January 1948. Yet there is no known recording of his transmissions, no surviving verbatim transcript, and no contemporary document that conclusively proves he used those exact words. What survives instead is a collection of witness recollections, later summaries and retellings that do not always agree. As a result, the reliability of Mantell’s final radio reports is a central question in understanding the incident. The evidence suggests that Mantell did report an object above him and continued climbing to investigate it, but the most dramatic wording associated with the case is much less certain than popular accounts often imply.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

## What Did Thomas Mantell Really Say?

Several elements of Mantell’s radio communications appear consistently across different accounts. He informed observers that he had sighted the object, that it was above him, and that he was climbing in an attempt to approach it. One version, preserved in later summaries of the investigation, quotes him as reporting: “The object is directly ahead of me and above me now,” while noting that it appeared to be moving more slowly than his aircraft. Similar versions also include his statement that he was still climbing and trying to get a better look.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dvidshub.net/news/436733/questions-remain-75-years-after-mysterious-fort-knox-ufo-incident-downed-pilot" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dvidshub.net">[DVIDS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dvidshub.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DVIDSQuestions remain 75 years after mysterious Fort Knox UFO...13 Jan 2023 — At 2:45 p.m., Mantell radioed the tower that he had spotte...</span></span></span>

The famous phrase about a “metallic object of tremendous size” is more problematic. It appears repeatedly in later UFO literature and became one of the defining quotations of the case. However, Air Force UFO investigator Edward J. Ruppelt later wrote that when he revisited the incident, personnel at Godman Army Airfield could not reliably remember Mantell making that specific statement. Ruppelt noted that UFO writers had attributed the phrase to Mantell, but the evidence for the exact wording was uncertain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

This distinction matters. There is a significant difference between:

* A well-supported claim that Mantell reported seeing an object and pursued it.
* A less secure claim that he specifically described it as a gigantic metallic craft.

The first is supported by multiple sources. The second rests on disputed recollections and later retellings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

## Why Witness Recollections Conflicted

A major obstacle to evaluating Mantell’s final reports is that no audio recording is known to exist. Godman Army Airfield did not preserve a recording of the radio traffic, and investigators largely relied on interviews conducted after the crash. Witnesses were therefore recalling conversations from memory rather than consulting an objective record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mysteryinksite.wordpress.com/2016/08/26/the-mantell-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mysteryinksite.wordpress.com">[Mystery Ink]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mysteryinksite.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the mantell mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mystery InkThe Mantell Mystery26 Aug 2016 — For example, there was no recording or formal transcription of radio messages between Godman...</span></span></span>

Several factors likely contributed to inconsistencies:

**The event became dramatic very quickly.** Mantell died only hours after the pursuit. Once the story became national news, memories may have been influenced by repeated discussion, media coverage and speculation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

**Different people heard different parts of the exchange.** Tower personnel, pilots and investigators were not all listening under identical conditions. Some may have heard only fragments of the conversation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mysteryinksite.wordpress.com/2016/08/26/the-mantell-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mysteryinksite.wordpress.com">[Mystery Ink]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mysteryinksite.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the mantell mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mystery InkThe Mantell Mystery26 Aug 2016 — For example, there was no recording or formal transcription of radio messages between Godman...</span></span></span>

**The object itself was difficult to describe.** Observers on the ground gave widely varying descriptions, comparing it to a balloon, a parachute, an ice-cream cone or a metallic object. If witnesses could not agree on what they were seeing, it is not surprising that later recollections of radio descriptions also differed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

These factors do not imply dishonesty. Rather, they illustrate a common problem in historical investigations: sincere witnesses can remember the same event differently, especially when no primary recording survives.

## How Later Retellings Changed the Case

The uncertainty surrounding Mantell’s words did not prevent the dramatic version from becoming dominant. As UFO interest expanded during the 1950s and later decades, the phrase “metallic object of tremendous size” became one of the most frequently quoted lines in American UFO history. It appeared in books, articles and television programmes as though it were a confirmed radio transcript.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sacred-texts.com/ufo/fsar/fsar03.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sacred-texts.com">[Internet Sacred Text Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sacred-texts.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Linked with the death of Mantell was the Lockbourne, Ohio, report,</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet Sacred Text ArchiveThe Flying Saucers Are Real: Chapter IIJuly 16, 1950 — It quoted Mantell&#x27;s radio report that the thing was me...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 16, 1950</span></span></span>

Over time, the quotation acquired a level of authority that exceeded the surviving evidence. In many retellings, readers are given the impression that investigators possessed a precise transcript or recording. Yet later examinations repeatedly returned to the same problem: the wording originated from recollections and reports whose chain of transmission is not entirely clear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

This process altered how the incident was understood. If Mantell merely reported an unidentified object above him, the case can be evaluated alongside the possibility that he was pursuing a high-altitude balloon. If, however, one accepts without question that he described a gigantic metallic craft, the incident appears more extraordinary. The reliability of the quotation therefore has direct implications for how persuasive the UFO interpretation seems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mantell UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mantell UFO incident</span></span></span>

    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
