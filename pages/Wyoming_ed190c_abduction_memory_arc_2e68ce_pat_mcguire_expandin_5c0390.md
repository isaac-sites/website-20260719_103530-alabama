## Introduction

Pat McGuire's story occupies a distinctive place in [Wyoming]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-wyomings-ufo/' | relative_url }})'s UFO history because it did not remain a single unexplained incident. What reportedly began as a confusing hunting trip in the Teton region in October 1973, involving an unexplained orange glow and several missing hours, gradually developed into one of the state's most elaborate contact narratives. Over the following years, McGuire described repeated UFO visits to his ranch near Bosler, encounters with human-like beings, unusual cattle deaths and messages from what he called the "Star People".<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2023/09/30/alien-abductions-of-2-wyoming-men-in-the-1970s-remain-unexplainable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">alien abductions of 2 wyoming men in the 1970s remain unexplainable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State DailyAlien Abductions Of 2 Wyoming Men In The 1970s Remain Unexplainable &#124; Cowboy State DailySeptember 30, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2023</span></span></span>

The case matters less because it offers strong physical evidence than because it illustrates how a personal mystery evolved into a much broader belief system. For readers interested in Wyoming's UFO history, McGuire's account is therefore significant both as a witness narrative and as an example of how extraordinary claims can expand over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2023/09/30/alien-abductions-of-2-wyoming-men-in-the-1970s-remain-unexplainable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">alien abductions of 2 wyoming men in the 1970s remain unexplainable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State DailyAlien Abductions Of 2 Wyoming Men In The 1970s Remain Unexplainable &#124; Cowboy State DailySeptember 30, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2023</span></span></span>

## The 1973 hunting trip and the missing hours

According to McGuire's later recollections and family accounts, the story began during an elk hunting trip in the Teton Mountains in early October 1973. He and his brother-in-law reportedly became lost, noticed an unusual orange glow, and eventually returned to their vehicle unable to explain several hours of elapsed time. At the time, the experience appears to have been remembered primarily as strange and unsettling rather than as an alien encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2023/09/30/alien-abductions-of-2-wyoming-men-in-the-1970s-remain-unexplainable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">alien abductions of 2 wyoming men in the 1970s remain unexplainable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State DailyAlien Abductions Of 2 Wyoming Men In The 1970s Remain Unexplainable &#124; Cowboy State DailySeptember 30, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2023</span></span></span>

## How the ranch encounters expanded the account

The most dramatic elements of McGuire's story emerged several years after the hunting trip and centred on his ranch east of Bosler.

Beginning in 1976, McGuire reported finding cattle dead under unusual circumstances. He initially suspected human activity rather than extraterrestrials, believing local individuals might be responsible for the mutilations. After camping out to watch his livestock, however, he and other witnesses claimed to have seen bright lights moving across the ranch. These reports gradually evolved into descriptions of structured craft hovering low over the property.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2023/09/30/alien-abductions-of-2-wyoming-men-in-the-1970s-remain-unexplainable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">alien abductions of 2 wyoming men in the 1970s remain unexplainable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State DailyAlien Abductions Of 2 Wyoming Men In The 1970s Remain Unexplainable &#124; Cowboy State DailySeptember 30, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2023</span></span></span>

McGuire later stated that:

* multiple craft repeatedly visited the ranch;
* orange lights illuminated the surrounding landscape;
* occupants could be seen moving inside landed objects;
* one witness appeared to walk towards a craft while seemingly in a trance;
* attempts to photograph the events with Polaroid cameras failed;
* the visits occurred dozens of times over several years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2023/09/30/alien-abductions-of-2-wyoming-men-in-the-1970s-remain-unexplainable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">alien abductions of 2 wyoming men in the 1970s remain unexplainable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State DailyAlien Abductions Of 2 Wyoming Men In The 1970s Remain Unexplainable &#124; Cowboy State DailySeptember 30, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2023</span></span></span>

These claims represented a substantial expansion from the original hunting incident. Instead of a single unexplained episode, McGuire now described an ongoing relationship with non-human visitors that supposedly affected both his family and his ranching operation.

In 1976 he submitted reports to the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization (APRO), one of the leading civilian UFO groups of the period. This established a documentary record showing that McGuire was actively reporting repeated experiences rather than recounting them only decades later. However, APRO's acceptance of a report should not be confused with independent verification of the events themselves.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2023/09/30/alien-abductions-of-2-wyoming-men-in-the-1970s-remain-unexplainable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">alien abductions of 2 wyoming men in the 1970s remain unexplainable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State DailyAlien Abductions Of 2 Wyoming Men In The 1970s Remain Unexplainable &#124; Cowboy State DailySeptember 30, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2023</span></span></span>

## From visitors to "Star People"

Another significant shift occurred after McGuire became associated with University of Wyoming psychologist and UFO investigator Leo Sprinkle, who conducted multiple hypnotic sessions with him.

During and after these sessions, McGuire described increasingly detailed encounters with human-like beings. He referred to them as "Star People", gave physical descriptions of their appearance and claimed they communicated warnings and instructions concerning humanity's future. Later accounts also included guidance about finding water on his ranch and broader prophetic messages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dragonattheendoftime.com/patrick-mcguire-experiencer-alien-encounters-contactee-abductee/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dragonattheendoftime.com">[Dragon at the End of Time]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dragonattheendoftime.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dragon at the End of TimePATRICK McGUIRE ~ Experiencer, Alien Encounters, Contactee, Abductee – Dragon at the End of TimeJune 22, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 22, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why many researchers remain cautious

McGuire's story is best understood as a layered narrative rather than a single documented incident.

Several factors encourage caution.

First, the most extraordinary claims appeared after the original hunting experience rather than immediately following it. This makes it difficult to distinguish between memories recorded close to the events and details added through later interpretation.

Second, hypnosis played a significant role in expanding the account. Modern psychological research generally finds that hypnosis can increase the amount of remembered detail and the witness's confidence in those memories without reliably demonstrating that the additional recollections are historically accurate. This does not imply deliberate fabrication, but it does limit the evidential weight that can be placed on hypnotically recovered memories.

Third, many dramatic claims—including repeated landings, conversations with non-human beings and prophetic messages—depend almost entirely on testimony rather than independently testable evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dragonattheendoftime.com/patrick-mcguire-experiencer-alien-encounters-contactee-abductee/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dragonattheendoftime.com">[dragonattheendoftime.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dragonattheendoftime.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dragon at the End of TimePATRICK McGUIRE ~ Experiencer, Alien Encounters, Contactee, Abductee – Dragon at the End of TimeJune 22, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 22, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why the case remains important in Wyoming's UFO history

Pat McGuire's account remains one of Wyoming's best-known contact narratives not because it has been conclusively verified, but because it demonstrates how an apparently simple mystery developed into an extensive body of extraordinary claims.

Within the broader history of Wyoming UFO reports, the case links several recurring themes of the 1970s: missing time, unusual lights, cattle mutilation reports, civilian UFO investigation organisations and hypnosis-based memory recovery. It also provides a useful comparison with the better-known Carl [Higdon case]({{ 'higdon-case/' | relative_url }}).
