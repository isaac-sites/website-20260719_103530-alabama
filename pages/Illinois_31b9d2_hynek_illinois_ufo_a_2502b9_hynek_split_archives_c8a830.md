## Introduction

Anyone trying to reconstruct J. Allen Hynek’s UFO investigations from [Illinois]({{ 'why-illinois-became-a-ufo-hotspot/' | relative_url }}) archives quickly encounters a problem: there is no single, complete Hynek archive. His surviving records are divided between Northwestern University, where he spent much of his academic career in Evanston, and the Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS), the private research organisation he founded in Illinois in 1973. This split matters because Hynek occupies a central place in Illinois UFO history. His papers are often treated as a key source for understanding both famous sightings and the broader shift from Air Force-sponsored investigations to civilian UFO research. Yet the surviving record is fragmented by institutional boundaries, different cataloguing practices and decades of separate stewardship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/resources/373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen Hynek (1910-1986) PapersThe 1972 publication of Hynek&#x27;s book, The UFO Experience, the Oct. 1973 UFO sightings in Mississippi, the 1...</span></span></span>

## What Northwestern Preserves

Northwestern University's archival collection preserves a substantial record of Hynek's professional career. The university's finding aid describes papers covering the period from the 1920s through the late 1980s, including material related to his astronomy work, departmental responsibilities, media coverage, Project Stargazer and selected UFO-related files. The collection also documents the years when Hynek became a prominent public figure through books, lectures and media appearances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/resources/373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen Hynek (1910-1986) PapersThe 1972 publication of Hynek&#x27;s book, The UFO Experience, the Oct. 1973 UFO sightings in Mississippi, the 1...</span></span></span>

For historians, Northwestern's collection is valuable because it places UFO research within the context of Hynek's wider scientific career. It shows that UFOs were only one part of a professional life that included astronomy, satellite-tracking projects and university administration. This broader perspective helps counter the tendency to view Hynek solely through the lens of UFO culture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/agents/people/1473" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hynek, J</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen (Joseph Allen), 1910-1986Allen Hynek led Northwestern&#x27;s Astronomy Department into the Space Age and became the nation&#x27;s foremost ex...</span></span></span>

At the same time, Northwestern's own descriptions acknowledge important limitations. The collection contains only some UFO-related material and is not a comprehensive repository of Hynek's investigative files. University archivists note that the papers provide a record of his professional activities but contain significant gaps, including missing personal correspondence and other records that might illuminate how particular conclusions were reached.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/archival_objects/78628" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">archival objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO Studies, 1970sThe papers include biographical materials, clippings, reports relating to Hynek&#x27;s work on Project Stargazer...</span></span></span>

## What Remains With the Center for UFO Studies

A different part of Hynek's legacy remained with CUFOS. Founded in Evanston during 1973, the organisation became Hynek's principal vehicle for civilian UFO research after the end of Project Blue Book. CUFOS continues to maintain portions of his archive, including correspondence, topic files, witness interviews and case-related notes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/resources/373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen Hynek (1910-1986) PapersThe 1972 publication of Hynek&#x27;s book, The UFO Experience, the Oct. 1973 UFO sightings in Mississippi, the 1...</span></span></span>

CUFOS has publicly described preserving original filing cabinets and collections containing material on specific investigations, debates, publications and recurring UFO themes. These holdings often contain the kind of working documents that historians seek when trying to understand how a case was evaluated rather than merely how it was presented to the public.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/hynek-biography/hynek-archives/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cufos.org.</span></span></span>

The practical consequence is that some of the most detailed evidence about particular sightings may remain outside the university archive. A researcher consulting Northwestern alone may find references to investigations, media coverage or published conclusions without seeing all of the underlying witness statements, correspondence or research notes preserved by CUFOS.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/resources/373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen Hynek (1910-1986) PapersThe 1972 publication of Hynek&#x27;s book, The UFO Experience, the Oct. 1973 UFO sightings in Mississippi, the 1...</span></span></span>

## Why the Collections Became Divided

The division reflects the unusual position Hynek occupied between academia and private UFO research.

Northwestern was willing to preserve records associated with a distinguished astronomy professor, but UFO research occupied an awkward position within mainstream academic institutions during much of Hynek's career. Meanwhile, CUFOS was created specifically to pursue investigations that Hynek believed deserved continued attention after official Air Force programmes ended. The result was two institutional homes with different priorities: a university archive concerned with preserving the record of an academic career, and a specialised organisation focused on ongoing UFO research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/resources/373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen Hynek (1910-1986) PapersThe 1972 publication of Hynek&#x27;s book, The UFO Experience, the Oct. 1973 UFO sightings in Mississippi, the 1...</span></span></span>

This arrangement was not necessarily planned as a long-term archival strategy. Rather, records accumulated in different places as Hynek's work crossed boundaries between university science, public controversy, media attention and private research networks. Over time, those separate collections developed their own catalogues and access systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/6/resources/373" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: findingaids.library.northwestern.edu">[findingaids.library.northwestern.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">findingaids.library.northwestern.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Allen Hynek (1910-1986) PapersThe 1972 publication of Hynek&#x27;s book, The UFO Experience, the Oct. 1973 UFO sightings in Mississippi, the 1...</span></span></span>

## How Divided Collections Affect Historical Conclusions

The split archive creates several problems for anyone attempting to assess Illinois UFO history.

First, it can produce a false sense of completeness. Researchers may assume that a university archive contains all surviving records when, in reality, significant [case files]({{ 'case-files/' | relative_url }}) or correspondence may reside elsewhere.
